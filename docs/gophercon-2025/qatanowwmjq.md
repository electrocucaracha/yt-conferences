# Summary

Tom Lions, a member of the Go Review Committee, offers essential guidelines for crafting successful talk proposals for the Go conference.
He advises speakers to submit well-crafted, concise proposals that are deeply rooted in the Go language and its ecosystem, featuring detailed outlines, concrete examples, and a clear explanation of why they are the ideal presenter.
Proposals must demonstrate professional tone, precision in scope and timing, and verified accuracy, while clearly stating the intended impact on the audience.
Conversely, speakers should avoid topics that stray from Go, such as general professional development or product sales, and refrain from submitting content that is better suited for blog posts or contains unprofessional elements like emojis and slang.
By adhering to these standards of expertise, passion, and professionalism, speakers can create engaging presentations that inspire the community and leave a lasting impression.
