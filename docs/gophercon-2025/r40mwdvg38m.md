# Summary

The panel, featuring experts from Google, Anthropic, and other tech firms, advises Go developers to cut through AI hype by focusing on practical experimentation with specific tools like agentic CLIs and MCP servers rather than trying to master every new technology.
Participants emphasize that Go remains highly relevant for building production-grade infrastructure, as its strengths in concurrency, reliability, and deterministic validation complement AI’s capabilities for code generation and orchestration.
Regarding career concerns, the consensus is that while AI automates routine coding tasks, it elevates the importance of fundamental engineering skills such as system design, problem-solving, and understanding complex integrations, ensuring that human expertise remains critical.
The group also addresses energy efficiency, noting that while current LLMs are resource-intensive, the industry is in a discovery phase where optimization, specialized hardware, and smaller distilled models will eventually improve sustainability.
Finally, the speakers urge engineers to remain open to learning, avoid embarrassment over knowledge gaps, and view this technological shift as a significant opportunity to build new infrastructure and drive innovation in the AI ecosystem.
