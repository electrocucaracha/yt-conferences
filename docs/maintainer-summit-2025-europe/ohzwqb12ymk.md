# Summary

This presentation outlines the new CNCF project review process, a sub-project under the Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) designed to streamline project maturity assessments and reduce review backlogs.
The session details specific technical and governance questions for three maturity stages: Day Zero (sandbox), focusing on design, architecture, and initial security; Day One (incubation), emphasizing configuration, activation, rollback, and production readiness; and Day Two (graduation), which requires rigorous documentation of scalability, observability, dependency management, and advanced security practices.
Governance reviews are highlighted as critical for long-term project health, with a strong recommendation to use CNCF templates—such as the maintainer council model—to establish clear, vendor-neutral decision-making processes that evolve with the project.
The new structure aims to increase velocity by integrating these reviews into the level-up application process, allowing projects to complete assessments like governance or technical reviews in lieu of certain due diligence sections, while also encouraging broader community involvement through tag representatives and subject matter experts.
