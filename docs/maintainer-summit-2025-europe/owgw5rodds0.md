# Summary

During the closing of the CNCF Maintainer Summit, the organizers announced the winner of the initiative workshop, "Orange is the New Black," which proposed a resource catalog and observability tool to help projects manage cloud costs and infrastructure more efficiently.
The team expressed gratitude to sponsors such as GitHub, Red Hat, and Microsoft Azure, while specifically highlighting the critical support provided by the CNCF events team, including individuals named Linday, Caroline, Ji, and Bob.
Attendees were strongly encouraged to complete a feedback survey to ensure the continued success and improvement of future summits, as the event had sold out and received positive community response.
The next large-scale Maintainer Summit is scheduled for Atlanta, with the call for proposals opening on May 19 and closing at the end of July to allow ample time for submissions before summer vacations.
Additionally, smaller maintainer-focused sessions will be integrated into other KubeCon events in China, India, and Japan, and the organizers are actively seeking new program chairs to lead the summits in 2026 and 2027.
