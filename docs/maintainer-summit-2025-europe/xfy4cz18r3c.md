# Summary

The CNCF Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) introduced its 2025 and 2024 members, including a new shadow program designed to ensure continuity and support for the committee.
The TOC clarified its role in defining the technical vision, approving project progression through sandbox, incubation, and graduation levels, and maintaining governance standards, while emphasizing that technical decisions remain with individual project steering committees.
To improve efficiency and transparency, the TOC announced a "reset" for the coming year that includes stricter application criteria, a focus on standardization, and a restructuring of Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs) to address leadership burnout and expand domain expertise.
Speakers encouraged project maintainers to utilize public issue trackers for guidance, conduct self-reviews against declarative criteria, and engage with TAGs for feedback, noting that sandbox applications no longer require a TOC sponsor.
The session concluded with an invitation for attendees to participate in the upcoming Tag Reboot workshop and elections for new TAG leadership positions following KubeCon.

# Main Points

|   # | Main point                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| --: | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|   1 | The CNCF Technical Oversight Committee (TOC) is elected by the community, distinct from the sponsorship-based Governing Board, and handles technical decisions while the board manages legal and financial matters.     |
|   2 | A new TOC shadow program has been introduced for 2025, allowing two members to participate in discussions and shadow TOC work for one year to prepare for potential leadership roles.                                   |
|   3 | The TOC defines and maintains the technical vision, approves new projects, and manages the progression of projects from sandbox to incubation to graduation levels.                                                     |
|   4 | The committee maintains the code of conduct, implements best practices, and coordinates with CNCF staff to provide services that maximize project team productivity.                                                    |
|   5 | Individual project governance, such as the Kubernetes Steering Committee, handles day-to-day non-technical decisions and interfaces with the TOC for collaboration or specific requests.                                |
|   6 | The TOC aims to address issues with projects not being ready for level transitions by implementing stricter application criteria and engaging with projects that have remained in incubation for extended periods.      |
|   7 | The committee is focusing on standardization, consistency, and transparency to ensure unbiased due diligence processes and to help projects reach maturity faster.                                                      |
|   8 | Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs) provide guidance on contributor strategy, project health, and governance, while also resolving technical and intellectual property challenges behind the scenes.                       |
|   9 | The TOC coordinates between different projects, dependencies, and the end user TAG to ensure roadmaps align with community needs and best practices.                                                                    |
|  10 | Graduation and incubation criteria have been made more declarative and transparent, with all discussions and successful examples publicly available in issues and pull requests to eliminate back-channel negotiations. |
|  11 | Project maintainers are encouraged to review public examples of successful applications, conduct self-reviews against criteria, and reach out to TAG members for feedback during the application process.               |
|  12 | The TOC is not a full-time body, so application review times vary; maintainers should be aware of the KubeCon freeze period, which starts four weeks before and ends two weeks after the event.                         |
|  13 | A "Tag Reboot" initiative is underway to restructure Technical Advisory Groups, address leadership burnout, and expand the pool of domain experts through new sub-project initiatives.                                  |
|  14 | Elections for new TAG chairs and technical leads will occur shortly after KubeCon, with opportunities for community members to take on leadership roles.                                                                |
|  15 | Sandbox project reviews are now faster and no longer require a TOC sponsor; applicants only need to fill out the standard template to submit their project.                                                             |
|  16 | Participation in TAGs is recommended for those interested in growing their visibility within the CNCF, as it can serve as a pathway to becoming a TOC member.                                                           |
|  17 | A Tag Reboot workshop will be held in Room 17 to generate ideas for collaboration and interoperability, featuring a contest with prizes for participants.                                                               |
