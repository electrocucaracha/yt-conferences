# Summary

Mario and Chris, co-chairs of the Kubernetes SIG Contributor Communications (Comms) team, discuss the evolution of project communications from informal community meetings to a structured team focused on messaging, social media, and SEO.
They highlight the critical importance of clear language, citing the "Docker Shim" deprecation incident as a case study where ambiguous terminology led to widespread misunderstanding and FUD, demonstrating how simple words can significantly impact community perception.
The speakers emphasize that effective communication requires tailoring content to specific platforms, such as LinkedIn for professional updates and Twitter for broader engagement, while leveraging automation tools like Buffer to manage posting schedules and reduce manual workload.
They also outline strategies for onboarding new contributors through low-barrier tasks like writing spotlights and managing critical communications, such as the Kubernetes registry change, by proactively reaching out to vendors to prevent cluster outages.
Finally, they advise projects to define clear social media policies, select channels based on where their audience actually resides, and utilize CNCF resources for funding and guidelines to maintain consistent, inclusive, and effective outreach.
