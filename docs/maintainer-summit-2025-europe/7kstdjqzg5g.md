# Summary

The second Maintainer Summit, the first held in Europe, was co-chaired by Mario, Natalie, and Karina to bring together maintainers from across the CNCF ecosystem, extending the traditional Kubernetes contributor summits to include sandbox, incubating, and graduated projects.
The event, sponsored by GitHub, Red Hat, and Microsoft Azure, emphasized a code of conduct focused on inclusivity and provided logistical support such as Wi-Fi, project meeting rooms, and catering.
Natalie explained that the summit aims to strengthen the CNCF community by allowing maintainers to coordinate across projects, understand the CNCF’s operational structure, and engage in both curated talks and participant-driven unconference sessions.
The organizers highlighted the importance of networking and face-to-face interaction to build stronger relationships among maintainers, while also soliciting feedback through surveys to improve future events.
The summit concluded with a call for participants to actively engage in discussions, submit unconference topics, and connect with peers to foster a more cohesive and effective open-source community.
