# Summary

Sustaining graduated open source projects requires proactive measures to address common challenges such as maintainer burnout, governance complexity, and contributor retention.
The speaker emphasizes that effective governance should be established during periods of stability rather than in crisis, recommending the simplest possible models like the maintainer council to minimize overhead while ensuring clear decision-making processes.
To grow the contributor base, projects should implement a "contributor ladder" that clearly defines roles and responsibilities, facilitating the promotion of new contributors into leadership positions and reducing the burden on existing maintainers.
Additionally, maintaining vendor neutrality is critical for CNCF projects to ensure organizational diversity, mitigate adoption risks, and prevent reliance on a single company’s resources.
Finally, improving responsiveness to pull requests and issues, along with maintaining up-to-date onboarding documentation and roadmaps, are essential strategies for building trust, retaining contributors, and ensuring the long-term health and sustainability of the project.
