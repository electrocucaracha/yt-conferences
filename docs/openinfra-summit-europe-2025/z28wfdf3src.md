# Summary

Yaya from the N Group Open Source Team presents an analysis of AI infrastructure trends derived from open source community data, highlighting how AI has surpassed other technologies to become the most influential sector since 2023.
The presentation identifies three key architectural trends: the scaling of parameters via Mixture of Experts, the integration of reinforcement learning to enhance reasoning capabilities, and the mainstream adoption of multimodal models.
By analyzing 114 prominent open source projects, the report reveals that Python and TypeScript form the ecosystem's backbone, with PyTorch emerging as the undisputed leader in model training and serving, significantly outpacing TensorFlow due to its flexibility and developer-friendly design.
The analysis further notes that while major tech giants and academic labs drive core infrastructure, smaller teams are innovating rapidly in the application layer, creating a dynamic landscape where projects like vLLM and SGLang lead in high-performance inference.
Ultimately, the N Group leverages these data-driven insights to guide corporate technology strategy, aiming to identify high-value projects for adoption and long-term investment while fostering broader community collaboration.
