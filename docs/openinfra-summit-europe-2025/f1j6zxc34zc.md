# Summary

Amy Marish, an OpenStack contributor and co-chair of the Open Infra Foundation board, presented a workshop on contributing to the OpenStack community, stepping in for the Manila team.
She emphasized that OpenStack powers critical global infrastructure, including telecommunications and cloud services, and highlighted the importance of diverse perspectives in identifying and resolving issues.
The session focused on lowering barriers to entry by guiding participants through the technical setup required for contribution, specifically using Gerrit and Git rather than GitHub, and explaining the community’s trust-building processes such as code reviews and bug reporting.
Marish demonstrated how to join the Open Infra Foundation, register on Gerrit, configure SSH keys, and utilize the DCO signing process, while also directing attendees to "low-hanging fruit" bugs on Launchpad for immediate practice.
Additionally, she outlined the various ways to contribute beyond coding, including documentation, testing, advocacy, and attending Project Team Gatherings (PTGs), which serve as the primary venue for collaborative work and discussion within the project.
