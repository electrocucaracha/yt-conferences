# Summary

The session focused on gathering feedback for the recently published "OpenStack for AI" white paper and determining the future direction of the Open Infra for AI working group.
Although few attendees had read the document, the group discussed potential next steps, including creating a similar white paper focused on Kata containers for securing AI workloads and developing case studies that highlight the synergy between Linux, OpenStack, and Kubernetes.
Participants emphasized the importance of efficient and sustainable AI inference, noting that optimizing GPU usage and leveraging low-latency infrastructure are critical for performance and green computing.
The discussion also highlighted opportunities for collaboration with the CNCF to align efforts on AI gateways and distributed inference, while acknowledging the need for more volunteers to participate in upcoming "show and tell" sessions.
Finally, the group agreed to explore integrating AI-specific modules into OpenStack services and encouraged community participation in the upcoming Project Teams Gathering to address standardization topics like MCP.
