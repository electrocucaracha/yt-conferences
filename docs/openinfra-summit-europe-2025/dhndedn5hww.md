# Summary

Nicholas, a field sales representative at Canonical, presents a discussion on the critical importance of digital sovereignty for European infrastructure, emphasizing the need to reduce dependency on foreign hyperscalers and proprietary vendors.
He argues that open source is the primary solution for achieving this independence, as it allows organizations to own their intellectual property, control their data, and significantly lower total costs of ownership while accessing state-of-the-art technology.
Canonical supports this transition by providing enterprise-grade tools and frameworks that automate the deployment of private clouds, Kubernetes, and databases, ensuring that users can maintain and scale their infrastructure without vendor lock-in.
The presentation highlights that by 2028, 68% of governments worldwide are expected to implement technological sovereignty mandates, making the ability to self-manage open source stacks a strategic necessity for enterprises and public sectors alike.
Canonical’s approach involves applying consistent automation and long-term security support across the entire stack, from bare-metal hardware to the application layer, enabling clients like the European Space Agency to run long-duration missions with reduced IT spend and greater operational autonomy.
