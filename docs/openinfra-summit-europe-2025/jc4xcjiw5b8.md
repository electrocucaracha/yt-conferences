# Summary

Adrian welcomes attendees to the Open Infra Summit Europe 2025 in Paris, highlighting the French community’s journey from a 2012 bar meetup to hosting the 2014 OpenStack Summit and now this major event.
He expresses gratitude to the foundation, volunteers, and sponsors, particularly OVH Cloud, for making the summit possible.
The speaker outlines key logistical details, including a post-keynote marketplace mixer, t-shirt pickup locations, and a free shuttle service between the train station and the campus.
Attendees are encouraged to explore the Open Source Pavilion for networking and hiring opportunities, participate in the VM Migration Day on Saturday, and engage with the colocateed G User Summit.
Finally, Adrian reminds participants to scan the QR code for the full schedule, provide feedback, and adhere to the event’s code of conduct while utilizing the help desk for any assistance.
