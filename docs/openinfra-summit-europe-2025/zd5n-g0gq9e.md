# Summary

The speaker highlights the growing adoption of AI workloads on OpenStack, noting that user survey data shows an increase from 24% in 2024 to 30% in 2025, alongside a milestone of 55 million cores in production.
To support this trend, the Open Infra AI working group has released a white paper titled "Open Infrastructure for AI," which details how OpenStack services handle AI scenarios and includes reference architectures from production environments such as 6G AI Sweden.
The presentation emphasizes the importance of resource allocation and data sovereignty, showcasing how multi-tenant isolation and bare-metal compute infrastructure are utilized to meet specific business needs.
The speaker encourages the community to provide feedback on areas for improvement, such as new features and sustainability, to ensure OpenStack remains a ubiquitous open-source infrastructure for AI.
Finally, the talk concludes with acknowledgments to platinum members like Rackspace, Huawei, and Koseno, and introduces a live demo by Koseno representatives Mike McDana and Rio Coyama.
