# Summary

The session, led by Gman, VP of Cloud Solutions at Cloud Flight, addresses the critical need for digital sovereignty and resilience in the face of tightening regulations like DORA and geopolitical shifts.
It highlights that relying on a single cloud provider creates significant ecosystem dependencies and risks, particularly for heavily regulated sectors such as banking and manufacturing, which require the ability to rapidly switch providers to maintain operational continuity.
To solve this, the presentation proposes a container-based abstraction layer utilizing managed Kubernetes clusters, allowing organizations to deploy standardized, automated infrastructure across multiple cloud providers—including hyperscalers and European alternatives—without reinventing the wheel.
A live demonstration showcased the system’s capability to automatically provision complex stacks, including IoT platforms, AI services, and workflow engines, on OVH Cloud in just 30 minutes, proving the feasibility of multi-cloud flexibility.
The discussion further emphasized that while European providers currently lack the scale of US hyperscalers, the strategic imperative for resilience, data portability, and regulatory compliance is driving a market shift toward flexible, multi-cloud architectures that ensure business survival against potential outages, legal blocks, or supply chain disruptions.
