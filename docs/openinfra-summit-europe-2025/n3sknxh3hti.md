# Summary

OVH, a 25-year-old infrastructure provider, has evolved from bare-metal hosting to a comprehensive public cloud platform, currently operating over 500,000 servers across 45 data centers.
The company’s strategy is deeply rooted in digital sovereignty, a vision driven by its founder’s background and a commitment to providing legal, fiscal, and physical isolation for customers who require high levels of data protection and control.
To meet these needs, OVH has developed its on-prem cloud platform, which allows for highly secure, scalable deployments ranging from single racks to large facilities, and is building a "cloud store" that integrates various technologies like VMware and OpenStack to simplify migration and management.
Regarding AI, OVH emphasizes that enterprise adoption is still in its early stages, requiring not just GPUs but robust infrastructure that ensures high speed, consistency, and security for sensitive data processing.
The company aims to empower enterprises to become their own cloud providers by offering tools that simplify access to compute and storage, addressing the growing demand for resilient, sovereign, and AI-ready infrastructure.
