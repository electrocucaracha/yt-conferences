# Summary

Delivered at the Open Infra Summit in Paris, this keynote argues that Europe should prioritize "resilience" over "digital sovereignty" to avoid the pitfalls of protectionism and isolation.
The speaker explains that open infrastructure, such as OpenStack and Kata Containers, provides the necessary resilience against geopolitical shifts, single-vendor dependencies like the VMware-Broadcom acquisition, and sophisticated security threats by enabling local, multi-vendor, and secure infrastructure.
Despite record-high adoption across critical sectors like banking, transportation, and research, the industry faces significant challenges, primarily a severe shortage of skilled infrastructure talent and the need to actively engage with incoming regulations.
To address these issues, the Open Infra Foundation has joined the Linux Foundation to strengthen policy advocacy and promote the "Open Infra Blueprint," which integrates Linux, OpenStack, and Kubernetes for end-to-end cloud-native solutions.
Ultimately, the speaker emphasizes that true resilience stems not just from technology, but from a global community working together beyond political borders.
