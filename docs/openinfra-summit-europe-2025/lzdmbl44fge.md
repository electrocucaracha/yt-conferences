# Summary

The CEO of Garg Forge introduces the company and its core product, Gerrit Code Review, highlighting its critical role in enabling large-scale collaboration for major open-source projects like OpenStack, Android, and Wikipedia.
He explains that Gerrit was originally developed by Google in 2008 to handle massive codebases and multi-company cooperation, a capability that distinguishes it from competitors like GitHub and GitLab.
The speaker emphasizes the strong partnership between Garg Forge and the OpenStack community, noting that key infrastructure components like Zuul and Gerrit are built on scalable, distributed technologies.
Addressing the current surge in AI-driven development, he discusses the resulting strain on software pipelines and repositories, proposing optimization strategies to manage these massive workloads efficiently.
Finally, he previews upcoming sessions featuring insights from GitHub’s founder, GitLab’s tech lead, and NVIDIA, who will share real-world experiences on optimizing infrastructure and integrating with Gerrit.
