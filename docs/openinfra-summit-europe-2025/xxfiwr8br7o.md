# Summary

Following Broadcom’s 2023 acquisition of VMware, which has led to significant price increases, reduced partner support, and licensing restrictions, analysts predict VMware will lose 30% of its market share by 2029.
In response, the Open Infra Foundation advocates for OpenStack as a robust alternative, emphasizing its adherence to the "four opens" principles and its collaborative ecosystem of users, developers, and partners.
Unlike single-vendor solutions such as Proxmox or Nutanix, which face scalability issues or similar vendor lock-in risks, OpenStack offers a healthy, community-driven infrastructure that has grown to 55 million production cores, with 64% of users running the majority of their infrastructure on the platform.
To facilitate the transition, over 30 companies collaborated on a VMware-to-OpenStack migration guide, while upstream developers have rapidly introduced new features to support GPU and AI workloads.
The Foundation is now opening its VMware migration working group to the broader community to gather user stories and feature requests, aiming to continuously adapt and support the evolving ecosystem over the next three to four years.
