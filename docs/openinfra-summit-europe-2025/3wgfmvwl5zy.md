# Summary

This session details the collaborative OpenStack contribution camp organized by the Indonesian and Korean user groups, emphasizing that active contribution extends beyond mere usage to include code, testing, documentation, and community support.
The initiative originated from a desire to connect Indonesian participants with global developers, leveraging the Korean group’s experience in running successful mentorship programs.
Preparations involved securing a free venue at a Jakarta university, establishing a dedicated OpenStack environment on bare metal, and selecting 20 mentees from students, lecturers, and enthusiasts based on their interest in open source and basic Linux knowledge.
The two-day curriculum, designed by the Korean mentor, focused on the Python OpenStack Client (CLI) project to teach essential skills such as Git/Gerrit workflows, PEP8 coding standards, and test case management, ensuring mentees could independently analyze and patch source code.
Results indicated that participants gained foundational knowledge and hands-on experience, while future plans include establishing a long-term mentorship program in Indonesia, expanding to other cities, and collaborating with user groups across different continents to support mentees in making their first upstream contributions.
