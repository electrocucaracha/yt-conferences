# Summary

The speaker introduces the Super User Awards, a program launched in 2014 to recognize organizations that innovate with and contribute back to open source software communities.
Five nominees are highlighted for their significant contributions: ECMWF for using open infrastructure in AI weather forecasting, G Research for managing data on an OpenStack-based cloud, StackHPC for being a major contributor to OpenStack releases, StackIT for creating open-source lifecycle management tools, and Telecom Paris for teaching OpenStack and maintaining high uptime.
After acknowledging the achievements of all nominees, the speaker announces StackHPC as the winner of the award.
The winning team is invited on stage for a group photo, and the speaker encourages them to stay for the celebration, which includes beer, champagne, and cupcakes.
