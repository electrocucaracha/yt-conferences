# Summary

The Maintainer Summit North America 2025 opened with welcome remarks from CNCF TOC Chair Karina Angel and program chairs Natalie Vlatco and Mario Farland, who thanked sponsors GitHub and Google for making the event possible.
The organizers emphasized the importance of adhering to the community code of conduct to ensure an inclusive and respectful environment, while also providing logistical details regarding Wi-Fi access, captioning services, and the schedule for lunch, the group photo, and the evening reception.
A significant portion of the address was dedicated to promoting the call for proposals for the upcoming summit in Amsterdam, with specific guidance urging maintainers to submit abstracts that focus on cross-project collaboration, transferable knowledge, and inclusivity rather than niche project promotion or reused content.
Daniel Crook from the CNCF projects team presented several new initiatives, including partnerships with GitHub Copilot, Okami, and Docker, the launch of LFX Insights version 4, and the development of a new CNCF chatbot and contribution site.
The session concluded with a request for attendees to complete the maintainer survey to help shape CNCF priorities for 2026 and an invitation for interested individuals to join the program committee for future summits.
