# Summary

The CNCF Technical Leadership Principles were developed to address conflicts and code of conduct violations arising from differing backgrounds and belief systems among contributors and maintainers.
These principles, available in the TOC repository and on the site, serve as guiding mechanisms for navigating uncertainty and fostering a sustainable, trusted ecosystem where leaders must inspire rather than rely on power dynamics.
The core framework for adopting these principles is "Pause, Acknowledge, and Engage," which encourages interrupting immediate emotional reactions, actively listening to validate others' perspectives, and maintaining constructive, public dialogue to ensure transparency and collective problem-solving.
The presentation highlights how failing to adhere to these principles can lead to severe community damage, such as the loss of contributors and project instability, whereas embracing them promotes empathy, critical thinking, and better technical outcomes.
Ultimately, the goal is to prioritize human connection and trust over code, ensuring that open source communities remain inclusive, resilient, and capable of producing high-quality technology through respectful collaboration.
