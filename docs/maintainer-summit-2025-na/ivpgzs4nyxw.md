# Summary

At the CNCF Maintainer Summit, the Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs) presented updates on their restructured initiatives and ongoing work to improve the cloud-native ecosystem.
The TAG Developer Experience, led by Daniel O., focused on enhancing developer workflows and AI integration by providing shared standards and guidelines for selecting and using CNCF projects, while also emphasizing collaboration with platform and DevOps engineers.
TAG Infrastructure, represented by Kashif Khan, highlighted its mission to define best practices for foundational resources like compute, storage, and networking, with active initiatives addressing infrastructure lifecycle management and data storage for AI workloads.
TAG Operational Resilience, chaired by Rafael Breita, outlined efforts to standardize software release processes and promote performance optimization, business continuity, and observability, introducing a "tardigrade" mascot to symbolize resilience.
Additionally, TAG Security and Compliance, led by Marina, detailed their focus on supply chain security insights, identity and access management, and providing security assessment frameworks for projects, while TAG Workloads Foundation, represented by Rajes, discussed standards for workload execution environments, including data locality, distributed agentic systems, and scheduling challenges for AI/ML workloads.
The session concluded with an invitation for maintainers to engage with these groups, participate in upcoming unconference sessions, and attend the one-year check-in in Amsterdam to review the progress of the CNCF’s technical landscape.
