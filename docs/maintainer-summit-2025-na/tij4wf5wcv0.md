# Summary

This tech workshop, continuing discussions from the Maintainer Summit in London, clarifies the distinction between Technical Community Groups (TCGs) and Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs), emphasizing that TCGs function like meetups that can apply for initiatives within TAGs.
The session explains that initiatives are time-bound artifacts that do not require working group applications or elections, with TAGs pre-approving their own initiatives post-KubeCon while limiting active initiatives to one tech lead each to ensure progress.
To manage stalled work, the TOC is implementing a "stalebot" to automatically archive inactive initiatives, removing emotional friction from the process and allowing maintainers to revisit them later when capacity permits.
Participants were directed to check the TOC repository board for initiative statuses and to update their progress, as labels for specific TAGs are still being implemented.
The workshop concluded with brief breakout sessions focused on involving projects in initiatives, defining initiative lifecycles, and improving the initiative sport, with notes to be shared in the TAG drive and further discussions scheduled for the next public TOC meeting.
