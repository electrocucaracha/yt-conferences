# Summary

The CNCF Developer Experience (DevX) tag, led by Daniel, Kevin, Julian, and other community members, aims to improve the productivity and workflow of developers working with cloud-native technologies.
The group focuses on creating shared standards, such as a "cloud native journey map," to help developers navigate the vast CNCF landscape and select appropriate tools for their specific needs without imposing a single "winner." Key initiatives include defining inner-loop workflows for AI engineers, specifying application integration dependencies, and establishing frameworks for secure coding that reduce friction for developers.
The tag distinguishes between short-term initiatives and longer-term sub-projects, such as enhancing developer experience for agentic AI and multi-architecture support, while actively collaborating with project maintainers to respect their priorities.
By engaging with the community and addressing pain points like documentation gaps and security integration, the DevX tag seeks to lower barriers to entry and foster a more accessible, efficient, and inclusive cloud-native development ecosystem.
