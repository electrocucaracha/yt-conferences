# Summary

At the CNCF Maintainer Summit, co-chairs Marina and Dylan Paige led an interactive session titled "Pathways Not Roadblocks" to discuss the recent restructuring of Technical Advisory Groups (TAGs) from eight to five specialized teams.
The discussion highlighted that the reboot was driven by concerns over leadership burnout, inconsistent domain expertise, and a lack of ongoing support for projects beyond their level transitions.
Audience members emphasized that discoverability remains a significant barrier, as many contributors and project maintainers struggle to find information about TAGs or understand how to engage with them effectively.
To address these challenges, the group proposed implementing a rotating liaison or "shadow" program to mentor new leaders, ensuring smoother succession and reducing the risk of sudden leadership gaps.
Furthermore, the speakers advocated for shorter, scoped initiatives to prevent burnout and encouraged a culture where volunteers feel empowered to contribute at any level without fear of overstepping.
The session concluded with the announcement of a new contributors.cncf.io site designed to improve discoverability and guide the community toward appropriate resources, reinforcing the goal of transforming TAGs from gatekeepers into supportive pathways for project growth.
