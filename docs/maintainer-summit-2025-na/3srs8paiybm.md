# Summary

The Kubernetes Steering Committee hosted an AMA session to address community concerns, beginning with an apology for the absence of several members due to travel and organizational issues.
The committee highlighted their progress in achieving greater diversity, thanking the community for voting for new members who better represent the project’s broad base.
A significant portion of the discussion focused on the recent Slack integration crisis; while the committee confirmed they will remain on Slack, they acknowledged that API changes require urgent rewriting of integrations and emphasized the importance of the newly formalized contract with the CNCF to ensure stability.
The conversation then shifted to the critical challenge of contributor burnout and the shrinking pool of active maintainers.
Committee members discussed the difficulty of finding replacements for leadership roles, noting that current leads are often spread too thin across multiple SIGs.
They proposed standardizing onboarding processes and contributor ladders across different Special Interest Groups to reduce intimidation for newcomers and to create a more consistent path for growth.
Additionally, the committee addressed the need for clear policies regarding the use of AI tools in code reviews and contributions, urging the community to define guidelines to prevent confusion and ensure fair evaluation of pull requests.
