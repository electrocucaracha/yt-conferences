# Summary

This panel discussion at PyCon features experts from various tech roles, including data science, marketing, and core Python development, who share their daily workflows for integrating AI tools like Claude Code, GitHub Copilot, and Codex into their work.
The speakers emphasize that AI significantly accelerates tasks such as coding, data cleaning, and content creation, allowing them to tackle complex problems and adjacent fields more efficiently than before.
However, they caution against the "agent for everything" mindset, advising that simple scripts often suffice and that users must remain the "driver" by maintaining human expertise to verify outputs, as AI models can hallucinate or provide suboptimal solutions.
The panelists also address the rapid pace of change in the AI landscape, suggesting that professionals should focus on learning tools that solve specific problems rather than chasing every new trend, while acknowledging the fatigue and anxiety this speed can cause.
Finally, they urge experienced developers to mentor newcomers to ensure the next generation of senior engineers is well-supported, highlighting the importance of human connection and ethical considerations, such as environmental impact and the responsible cleanup of AI-generated code.
