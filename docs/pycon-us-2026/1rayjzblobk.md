# Summary

Gu, a developer advocate at Microsoft, shares his personal journey of learning Python by leveraging the "best way to learn is to teach" principle.
He began by volunteering to translate his colleague’s English Python and AI content into Spanish, a process that forced him to deeply understand the material and provided him with concrete goals and deadlines.
As his confidence grew, he transitioned from translating to creating original examples and contributing to open-source projects, such as fixing bugs in the Microsoft AutoGen Framework, proving that small contributions are valuable.
Gu emphasizes the importance of using AI responsibly as a clarification tool rather than a code generator, and he highlights the power of community engagement, noting that their Spanish-language resources reached over 200,000 people through organic sharing by influencers and developers.
He advises newcomers to set clear goals, utilize quality books, and actively seek out community connections, asserting that one does not need to be an expert to contribute meaningfully to the Python ecosystem.
