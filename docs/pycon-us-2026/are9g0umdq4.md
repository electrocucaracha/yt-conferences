# Summary

The Python Steering Council, comprising five elected members, outlines their role in leading the core team, managing PSF funds, and supporting developers in residence through corporate sponsorships.
They highlight key features of Python 3.15, including lazy imports, frozen dictionaries, enhanced color output, and a new performance profiler, while urging the community to test the beta and provide feedback before the final release in October.
The council addresses community questions regarding the establishment of a new Packaging Council, the timeline for making free-threading the default, and the complex process of evaluating PEPs based on user impact and consensus.
Additionally, they discuss the potential integration of Rust into CPython, noting it is a multi-year effort requiring careful consideration of build processes and platform support.
Finally, the council emphasizes the importance of community contributions, such as the upcoming CPython sprint, and encourages ongoing engagement with the Python ecosystem.
