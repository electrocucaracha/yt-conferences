# Summary

The PSF Diversity and Inclusion (DNI) Work Group introduced its diverse, global membership, representing regions from North and South America to Africa, Asia, Europe, and Oceania.
Members shared personal narratives of how they joined the Python community, highlighting stories of overcoming financial barriers, learning in non-English languages, and finding mentorship through local initiatives like PyLadies and Django Girls.
The group outlined three core initiatives to support the community: creating a central hub for regional information, assisting with the establishment of local Python communities, and conducting surveys to gather feedback for continuous improvement.
These efforts are driven by the mission to ensure Python is accessible and welcoming to everyone, amplifying underrepresented voices and fostering inclusive spaces for new contributors worldwide.
