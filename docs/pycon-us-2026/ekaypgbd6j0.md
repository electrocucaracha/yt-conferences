# Summary

Paolo Melchiorre, a board member of the Python Software Foundation and organizer of PyCon Italia, presents a talk on the growing impact of AI-assisted contributions on open source maintainers.
He argues that while AI acts as an amplifier for both positive and negative aspects of code, it fundamentally changes the scale of open source by overwhelming volunteer resources with low-quality or unverified pull requests.
Melchiorre cites specific incidents, such as the sunset of the JetBrains project and aggressive bot behavior in matplotlib, to illustrate how AI can exacerbate maintainer fatigue and create new types of conflicts.
In response, various projects are implementing diverse policies ranging from requiring human verification to outright rejecting AI-generated code, highlighting the need for communities to establish clear norms.
The speaker concludes by advocating for increased human connection and collaboration, suggesting that fostering community bonds is the most effective way to navigate the challenges posed by AI, as it is the one element AI cannot replicate.
