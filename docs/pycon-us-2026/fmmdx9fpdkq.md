# Summary

Veronica Rodriguez, an Afro-Latina economist and self-taught product manager from Colombia, shares her journey of transitioning into the tech industry and addressing the significant underrepresentation of Black and Latino professionals in the field.
She highlights how Colombia, particularly Medellín, has become a major tech hub, yet she frequently noticed a lack of diversity in community events, realizing she was often the only person of color in the room.
Motivated by this exclusion and the realization that Colombian engineers are rarely depicted as Black or Indigenous, Rodriguez co-founded "Digital Diaspora," a nonprofit community in Medellín dedicated to increasing Afro-Colombian presence in technology.
The organization provides mentorship, bootcamps, and scholarships, partnering with companies like TransUnion and Platzi to offer training and job opportunities to underrepresented groups.
Rodriguez emphasizes the need for sponsorship over mere mentorship and advocates for inclusive design practices, arguing that diverse teams lead to better innovation, decision-making, and market expansion, while also working with government initiatives to ensure digital infrastructure access in Black regions.
