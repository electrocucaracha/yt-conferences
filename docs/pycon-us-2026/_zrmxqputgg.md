# Summary

Tim Schilling and Rachel Cahoun present Django Space, a mentorship program designed to solve the open source contributor pipeline and diversity problems by actively welcoming new developers into the Django community.
The eight-week program pairs mentees, known as "Jangonauts," with technical mentors called "Navigators" and community mentors called "Captains" to guide them through the complexities of contributing to Django, from setting up local environments to navigating pull request reviews.
By focusing on active inclusion and providing a supportive environment, the program has successfully onboarded over 100 contributors, with 100% of surveyed alumni recommending it and participants showing a significant increase in confidence and contribution rates.
The initiative has also fostered a strong pipeline of community leaders, with many alumni returning as volunteers, organizers, or board members, and has expanded its impact by supporting non-Django projects and providing financial aid for conference attendance.
Schilling and Cahoun emphasize that sustainable open source growth requires intentional community building, encouraging maintainers to participate in the program and individuals to apply, thereby creating a self-sustaining cycle of mentorship and inclusion.
