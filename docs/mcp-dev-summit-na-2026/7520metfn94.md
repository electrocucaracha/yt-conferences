# Summary

In this talk, Zev Clapau, a distinguished engineer at HubSpot, discusses how the company evolved its internal platform to support large-scale AI tool integration by building on its existing RPC framework, Chirp, rather than relying solely on MCP servers.
With over 2,000 AI tools now deployed across 510 services, HubSpot streamlined tool creation by introducing two simple annotations to its RPC system, allowing engineers to expose new tools to AI agents and MCP integrations with minimal friction and without deep protocol knowledge.
This approach centralized protocol complexity at the gateway layer, reduced barriers to adoption, minimized fragmentation, and enabled distributed ownership, with 97% of tools now owned by teams outside the core AI platform group.
The system also supports dynamic schema remapping and tool search, facilitating scalability and ease of use, and is now being extended to agent development through an internal library called Aviator.
While challenges remain around tool and agent design, the strategy of leveraging existing infrastructure and simplifying adoption has driven rapid growth and organic engagement in AI tooling across the organization.
