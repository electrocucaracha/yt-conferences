# Summary

The speaker reflects on the rapid growth of the MCP community since the first summit just over ten months ago, highlighting how the event has expanded from a small, single-track gathering to a much larger and more dynamic community.
They note the adoption of MCP by major companies like Amazon and Uber, as well as its role as an entry point for many startups and enterprises beginning their AI and agent journeys.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of MCP gateways and registries for providing control and governance within organizations, pointing out that many vendors now offer solutions in this space.
They discuss the evolving nature of agent development, with modern agents increasingly resembling skills or code generated by tools, and the growing significance of runtime, security, and governance layers—particularly in response to software supply chain risks and the need for robust access controls.
The talk concludes by noting that the architecture of MCP gateways and registries is advancing to address these new challenges, and the speaker invites attendees to visit their booth for more information.
