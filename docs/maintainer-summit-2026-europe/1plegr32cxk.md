# Summary

A CNCF staff member, formerly a Kubernetes maintainer, shares insights from their first nine months leading the end user program, emphasizing that the second decade of cloud native is defined by sophisticated end users who drive ecosystem growth.
The speaker highlights that end users are crucial for providing large-scale feedback and that their primary contributions should include attending community meetings, adding their logos to project adopter lists to support graduation processes, and collaborating on reference architectures.
To help maintainers leverage these relationships, the CNCF offers resources like LFX Insights and case studies to demonstrate the financial efficiency of open source, while also advising end users to strategically engage with projects through CubeCon and co-submitted CFPs.
The talk addresses challenges such as the lack of budget for full-time engineering support from end users and the difficulty of finding adopters for sandbox projects, suggesting that maintainers should look for related projects and vertical-specific communities to identify potential users.
Ultimately, the speaker advocates for professionalizing the relationship between maintainers and end users by creating dedicated spaces for collaboration, such as an end user track at CubeCon, and encourages maintainers to actively engage with the CNCF staff to facilitate introductions and access to hidden adopter data.
