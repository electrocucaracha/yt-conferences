# Summary

Arthur and Bartek, experienced maintainers of Prometheus and other CNCF projects, share their insights on growing contributors through mentorship programs like LFX and Google Summer of Code.
They emphasize that effective mentoring requires careful project scoping to ensure tasks are achievable within the program’s timeframe, as well as rigorous candidate selection processes that prioritize structured interviews over resume screening to assess genuine motivation and fit.
The speakers highlight the importance of co-mentoring to distribute workload and provide diverse perspectives, while also stressing the need for clear communication, regular feedback, and alignment with employers to sustain long-term engagement.
They discuss the challenges posed by AI-generated spam and low-quality contributions, advocating for a focus on interview-based selection and clear project boundaries to maintain community health.
Finally, they advise mentors to manage post-mentorship expectations by encouraging mentees to remain active in the community through other means, such as attending meetings or contributing to documentation, rather than relying on continued one-on-one support.
