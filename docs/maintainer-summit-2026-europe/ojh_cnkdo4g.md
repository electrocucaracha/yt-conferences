# Summary

This transcript details a CNCF sub-project meeting focused on establishing a reproducible process for re-evaluating graduated projects to ensure their continued health and growth.
The group utilized the Argo project as a case study, with core maintainer Dan Garfield explaining that Argo comprises four distinct sub-projects—CD, Workflows, Rollouts, and Events—that possess varying levels of maturity, security postures, and maintenance requirements.
To address the complexity of such multi-repository ecosystems, the team discussed the need for formal definitions of sub-projects and mechanisms to track their individual health, noting that current governance reviews often fail to capture the evolving nature of these projects over time.
Participants also explored the potential of the "dot project" initiative, a declarative YAML-based system for managing maintainer structures, as a tool to standardize governance documentation and automate the tracking of sub-project requirements.
The session concluded with a call to action for the community to engage with the project reviews channel and contribute to the development of these standardized evaluation templates.
