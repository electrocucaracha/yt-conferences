# Summary

Adriana Vella, Reese, and Josh discuss the evolution of the OpenTelemetry End User SIG, highlighting how it transitioned from a small working group into a permanent community SIG by addressing the gap between project maintainers and end users.
They emphasize that building a successful community requires intentionality, consistent engagement, and a welcoming culture that prioritizes kindness and vendor neutrality over aggressive sales tactics.
Key strategies for growth include hosting regular live streams like "Hotel Me" and "Hotel in Practice," maintaining transparency through public survey results and meeting recordings, and actively recruiting contributors to offset natural attrition.
The speakers note that while community building involves managing "forces of entropy" such as time and space constraints, consistency in showing up and providing value creates a sustainable foundation.
Ultimately, they advise that fostering a genuine, inclusive environment where members feel recognized and supported is the most effective way to retain participants and drive long-term project success.
