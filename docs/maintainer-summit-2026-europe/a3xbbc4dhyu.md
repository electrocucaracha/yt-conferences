# Summary

This panel discussion, moderated by Nate W.
of the CNCF, features documentation maintainers from Kubernetes, Grafana Labs, Bloomberg, and other projects who share insights on building effective open-source documentation teams.
The speakers emphasize the importance of establishing clear charters, fostering psychological safety, and granting contributors autonomy to ensure sustainable team growth.
They discuss practical mentorship strategies, such as the Kubernetes "PR Wrangler" shadowing program and the use of the LFX mentorship platform, to help new contributors navigate complex contribution pipelines and learn project-specific workflows.
A significant portion of the conversation addresses the challenges and opportunities presented by AI in documentation, including the need for clear etiquette guidelines, the risk of low-effort AI-generated pull requests, and the potential for AI to assist non-native English speakers in contributing technical knowledge.
Finally, the panel highlights the necessity of lowering barriers for new contributors by simplifying initial tasks, avoiding overly strict automated linting that discourages participation, and ensuring documentation remains accurate and accessible for both human readers and AI tools.
