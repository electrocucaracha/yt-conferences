# Summary

Sam Lesson emphasizes the importance of showing up with a low heart rate, being approachable, and having an abundance mindset.
Sam shares tips on introductions, small talk, conversations, and meeting etiquette, including not ordering the most expensive thing on the menu, using a strong handshake, and standing when people leave the table.
