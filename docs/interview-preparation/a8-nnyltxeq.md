# Summary

The top 1% of people use AI to enhance their intelligence by outsourcing tasks that require human interaction or judgment, such as customer interactions and product design, to AI-powered tools like drag framework, which categorizes work into four zones: drafting, research, analysis, and grunt work.
They also use AI to remove friction for information tasks and add friction for transformation tasks, effectively using AI as a spotter to help them learn and grow.
