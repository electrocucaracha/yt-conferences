# Summary

How to effectively prepare for technical interviews, particularly for senior roles.
He argues that most interview prep resources are geared towards junior and entry-level positions, focusing on coding problems, which is not sufficient for senior roles.
Instead, he recommends relying on proxies such as system design and leadership evaluation, as actual code design and leadership evaluation takes too long to assess.
The three levels of software engineering interviews are: Level 1 - Coding, Level 2 - System Design, and Level 3 - Leadership.
To prepare, allocate study time proportionally based on expectations, focusing on behavioral questions for leadership roles.
