# Summary

Down leveling occurs when companies extend an offer at a lower level than the candidate's actual potential, leading to discomfort and long-term consequences.
To avoid this, it's essential to understand that levels are relative across companies and to be honest with yourself about your experience and expectations.
Meta recommends using the "story shape" approach, which involves anchoring on status and responsibilities, layering in conflict and challenges, and demonstrating perspective on operating at a higher level.
By doing so, candidates can make it easy for interviewers to assess their level and increase their chances of getting hired at the right level.
