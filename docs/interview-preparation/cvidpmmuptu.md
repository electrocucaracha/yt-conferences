# Summary

The 1% elite use specific communication skills to sound powerful in boardrooms.
The five key skills include: (1) not explaining every detail, instead focusing on the headline; (2) delivering conviction through clear language and avoiding filler words; (3) using body language to convey confidence and presence; (4) telling stories that engage and inspire others; and (5) turning wins into "we" and losses into "me," prioritizing team success over personal achievements.
