# Summary

He identifies four universal traits: being technically deep, delivering on time, rejecting hyperbolic discounting, and growing others around them.
To achieve these traits, one must set direction, rally their team, and prioritize long-term consequences over immediate gains.
This requires extracting more time from existing working hours to think about the future, rather than relying on unsustainable work habits or putting in extra hours.
By growing others and investing in their development, senior engineers can create a positive impact on their teams and achieve success without sacrificing personal well-being.
