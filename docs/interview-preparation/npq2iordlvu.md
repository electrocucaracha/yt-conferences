# Summary

Learning how to learn faster than others.
The key to success lies in understanding why 99% of people fail at learning, which is due to their brain's limited capacity for processing information.
To overcome this, the speaker introduces the "3C protocol": compress (selecting and associating new ideas), compile (practicing and testing), and consolidate (retaining what has been learned).
By applying these techniques, individuals can break out of the ordinary and achieve mastery in their chosen field.
