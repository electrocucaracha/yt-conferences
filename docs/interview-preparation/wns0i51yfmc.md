# Summary

She recommends connecting with 20 people per day on LinkedIn, focusing on target companies and decision-makers, such as hiring managers or CEOs.
To increase the chances of getting a response, prioritize active members who post frequently and engage with others' content.
When reaching out to these individuals, send personalized messages highlighting why you're a good fit for the role, rather than sending generic resumes or AI-generated messages.
