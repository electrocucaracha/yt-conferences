# Summary

He reveals the reason for their stagnation was not due to their skills, but rather a series of unlearned assumptions that hindered their career growth.
These assumptions included believing that hard work alone would lead to recognition, thinking that becoming the best engineer in the room would guarantee advancement, and treating one's manager like any other stakeholder.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of shifting focus from individual achievements to contributing to others' success, making one's work visible, investing in relationships with managers, and taking ownership of one's outcomes.
By adopting a "high agency" mindset, individuals can break free from limitations and achieve their goals.
