# Summary

The most important question in an interview is not about your experience or skills, but rather how you answer "So, tell me about yourself." A counterintuitive strategy to make a lasting impression is to avoid repeating bullet points from your resume and instead connect with the interviewer on a personal level.
By using a simple narrative structure, starting with your current role, adding a flavor of how you operate, covering your past in a curated snapshot, and tying your expertise to the job description, you can showcase your personality and value without going off track.
