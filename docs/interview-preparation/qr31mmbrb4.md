# Summary

During an interview, candidates often look for subtle signs that indicate whether they've made a good impression.
A former recruiter shares five reliable signs that your interview actually went well: if the interviewer starts selling the company to you, gets specific about next steps, asks about your availability or notice period, and starts speaking like you already work there.
These signs reveal emotional certainty, logical confidence, and commitment from the hiring manager, which can increase the chances of getting an offer.
