# Summary

Austin McDonald, former hiring committee chair at Meta and author of "Mastering Behavioral Interviews," emphasizes the importance of behavioral interviews in assessing a candidate's skills and fit for a role.
He suggests that recruiters should focus on understanding what makes someone successful in their company culture and values, rather than just asking generic questions.
To prepare for behavioral interviews, candidates should identify core stories about their career experiences, practice delivering these stories with clarity and concision, and be prepared to adapt their delivery to fit the company's culture.
Additionally, recruiters can assess a candidate's experience with AI by evaluating their ownership, autonomy, and scope in projects, rather than asking specific questions about how they use AI.
