# Summary

When a recruiter asks about your job search, they're not just looking for information about your current role or salary expectations.
They want to know what's going on behind the scenes - who you're interviewing with, what stage you're at, and even the name of the hiring manager.
This is because recruiters are salespeople trying to fill roles fast, and sharing this intel can be a goldmine for them.
To protect yourself without sounding awkward or paranoid, consider responding with vague but casual answers (e.g., "I've got a few things progressing"), setting clear boundaries with empathy ("I'd never want to share my interview activity..."), or using reverse psychology ("Would you rather hold off on this one?").
