# Summary

Brilliant people get rejected and average candidates get offers despite lacking skills or experience.
The key to success lies in understanding human psychology, specifically cognitive ease, which refers to choosing what feels easy, and the halo effect, where likability can override technical qualifications.
By matching the interviewer's energy, being respectful, and showcasing authority through clarity, structure, and ownership, candidates can increase their chances of getting hired.
Additionally, building a strong personal brand online and using techniques like the commitment lock can further enhance their appeal to hiring managers.
