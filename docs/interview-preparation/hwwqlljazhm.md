# Summary

To manage time like the top 1%, you need to stop following traditional productivity advice and start using a system that rewires your brain.
The key is understanding your role, zone, and non-negotiable.
There are three roles: maker (individual contributor), marker (team leader managing multiple priorities), and multiplier (CEO or executive overseeing large teams).
Two zones exist: wartime (crisis mode) and peace time (stable period).
Your system must be dynamic, knowing which zone you're in to act accordingly.
The final piece is the one non-negotiable, focusing on a specific task that only you can do while delegating everything else.
Trust management is crucial, involving building trust with teammates through comfort, clarity, and context.
