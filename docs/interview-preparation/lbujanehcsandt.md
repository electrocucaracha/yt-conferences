# Summary

Preparing for interviews while holding a full-time job is a daunting task.
To tackle this, identify areas that need improvement and focus on those, rather than trying to cover everything.
Utilize your work schedule to prepare, such as listening to audiobooks during childcare duties or researching topics related to your current projects.
Leverage opportunities like performance reviews to highlight achievements and brush up on skills relevant to the job you're applying for.
Employ a "right-to-left" planning approach, working backwards from the interview date to ensure all necessary preparation is completed simultaneously.
