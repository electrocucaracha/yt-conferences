# Summary

Kate Chapman, a keynote speaker at PyCon US 2024, shares her journey from learning to program through books in childhood to working at the intersection of mapping and computer science.
After initially studying computer science but switching to geography due to a lack of fit, she developed the InaSAFE plugin for QGIS, a tool for disaster impact modeling.
Her inspiration for community engagement came from observing Tim Sutton’s inclusive Python sessions in Jakarta, which welcomed developers of all skill levels.
Chapman emphasizes her focus on solving socio-technical problems where access to essential resources like maps, knowledge, and supply chain transparency is often assumed to exist but does not.
She expresses excitement about connecting with the free and open-source software community at the conference to learn new technologies and collaborate on building inclusive systems.
