# Summary

Mariatta, the conference chair, welcomes attendees to PyCon US 2024 in Pittsburgh, highlighting a record-breaking year with nearly 1,000 call-for-paper proposals and over 3,000 registered participants, the largest turnout since the pandemic.
She expresses gratitude to the Python Software Foundation (PSF), volunteers, and sponsors, while outlining key logistical updates such as the new venue, the availability of the online experience for in-person attendees, and the introduction of hatchery programs like FlaskCon.
The PSF Executive Director, Deb Nicholson, provides an update on the organization’s mission, noting that Python is downloaded over 300 million times daily and that the PSF distributed nearly $700,000 in grants in 2023 to support community events and education.
Sponsors NVIDIA, AWS, and Fastly take the stage to discuss their contributions, including NVIDIA’s commitment to accelerating Python, AWS’s funding of a dedicated PyPI security role, and Fastly’s ten-year partnership with the PSF, which they have extended for another five years to continue serving critical infrastructure for the Python ecosystem.
