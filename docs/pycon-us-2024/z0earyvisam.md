# Summary

At PyCon US 2024, conference chair Mariatta opened the session by celebrating community milestones, including a record-breaking donation drive for Black Python Developers and her personal achievement of connecting with more Indonesian colleagues than in previous years.
She provided logistical updates regarding swag pickup, the expo hall’s poster sessions and job fair, and the upcoming development sprints, while also highlighting the availability of online talk recordings for attendees.
The keynote speaker, Kate Chapman, then addressed the theme of equitable opportunities in tech, arguing that many societal and technical problems are not truly "solved" because they lack base-level data and inclusive collaboration.
Chapman illustrated her points with examples from her work in humanitarian mapping, such as OpenStreetMap’s response to the Haiti earthquake and disaster preparedness in Indonesia, emphasizing that successful digital commons require motivation, timing, emergence, and additive culture.
She concluded by discussing her current work at Open Supply Hub, which aims to crowdsource supply chain transparency to address issues like forced labor and deforestation, asserting that building accessible digital public infrastructure is essential for creating a more equitable world.
