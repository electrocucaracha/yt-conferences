# Summary

At PyCon US, speakers Chuck, Tiasa, Marata, and Jai presented their podcast, "The Hidden Figures of Python," which aims to address the significant underrepresentation of women in the Python community.
While PyCon events have seen a substantial increase in female speakers, a study conducted by Marata revealed that only 1.7% of episodes across three major, established Python podcasts feature women as guests, highlighting a stark disparity in the podcasting space.
The team launched their series to spotlight underrepresented voices and ensure guests feel safe and comfortable, implementing practices such as pre-recording meetings, optional live streaming, and final content reviews to mitigate harassment and build trust.
They have already released four episodes featuring diverse contributors from various regions, including Colombia and Ireland, with a fifth episode planned for the APAC region.
The speakers encouraged the audience to support the initiative by sharing episodes, becoming allies, and donating to the Python Software Foundation, while also discussing strategies for safely engaging with women in tech who may be hesitant to participate due to past experiences of harassment.
