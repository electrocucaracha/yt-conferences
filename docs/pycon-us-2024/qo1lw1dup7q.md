# Summary

Simon, a keynote speaker at Python US with over 20 years of experience, recounts the origins of Django, which began as a tool for a Kansas newspaper to meet tight journalism deadlines and evolved into a major open-source web framework.
He highlights his enduring passion for Python’s interactive interpreter, which enabled rapid iterative development, and notes that this preference for immediate feedback influenced his appreciation for other languages with strong REPL experiences.
Currently, Simon focuses on building responsible tooling for large language models, specifically addressing the challenges of using AI in data journalism where accuracy is critical.
He emphasizes the importance of the Python community leveraging its influence to ensure these technologies have a positive impact on the world.
Finally, he encourages attendees to engage with software maintainers and values the perspective of newcomers, arguing that a beginner’s mindset is essential for improving libraries, documentation, and the overall community.
