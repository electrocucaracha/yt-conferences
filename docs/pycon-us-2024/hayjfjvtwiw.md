# Summary

Ian, a senior software engineer from Australia, presents a method for eliminating human error in mathematical assignments by using Python, SymPy, and Jupyter notebooks to "eat your homework." He argues that traditional methods like handwriting or manual LaTeX coding are prone to mistakes and tedious, whereas a programmatic approach allows for precise, reproducible, and visually clear working out.
The talk demonstrates solving discrete math, calculus, and optimization problems by leveraging SymPy’s symbolic capabilities to display step-by-step derivations, calculate exact solutions, and generate plots, effectively creating a submission-ready document that shows both the logic and the final answer.
Ian emphasizes that this workflow not only ensures accuracy but also deepens understanding by forcing the user to implement mathematical theorems as algorithms, contrasting this with AI tools that may simply provide answers without educational value.
He concludes by encouraging developers to contribute to SymPy and highlighting the utility of exporting Jupyter notebooks as HTML or PDF for professional submission.
