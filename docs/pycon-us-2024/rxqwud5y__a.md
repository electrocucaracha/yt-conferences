# Summary

Lynn Root, chair of the PyLadies Global Council, introduced the inaugural Outstanding PyLady Awards, recognizing Abigail Dogbe, Jessica Greene, and Maaya Ishida for their significant contributions to the Python community and their efforts in inspiring diverse participation.
Root highlighted that PyLadies raised a record-breaking $60,000 at the recent PyCon fundraising auction, funds that are primarily used to support underrepresented individuals in attending PyCon US and accessing professional opportunities.
Following this, the PSF Executive Director provided an update on the organization’s strategic initiatives, including the hiring of new CPython developers, increased investment in security and PyPI support, and the appointment of new communications and event management staff.
The director also discussed the PSF’s engagement with European legislation regarding open source security and encouraged community members to participate in the upcoming board elections and nominate candidates for community service awards.
The presentation concluded with heartfelt acknowledgments to the conference sponsors, the dedicated PSF staff, and Olivia Sauls for her crucial role in ensuring the success of PyCon US.
