# Summary

In this session, Mariata, the conference chair for PyCon US 2024, and Philippe, the program committee chair, discuss the talk selection process and provide guidance for potential speakers.
They explain that PyCon US features a main talk track consisting of 25-minute and 45-minute slots, with the longer slots reserved for highly competitive, community-critical topics.
The submission process requires a title, abstract, description, and a detailed outline with rough timings, which the review committee considers the most critical component for evaluation.
The selection process is a two-step procedure: an initial blind review based on proposal quality, followed by a non-blind stage where talks are categorized and consensus is reached to build a balanced program.
The speakers emphasize that PyCon US is a community-focused event, welcoming submissions from beginners and advanced users alike, while noting that commercial topics are better suited for sponsorship opportunities.
To assist first-time speakers, they highlight the availability of a mentorship program through the Python Software Foundation (PSF) and recommend reviewing examples of accepted and rejected proposals on the PyCon site.
Additionally, they clarify that speakers do not receive payment by default but can apply for travel grants and financial aid to cover costs.
The session concludes with reminders about the call for proposals deadline and other ways to participate, such as tutorials, sprints, and on-site volunteering.
