# Summary

Su Harihareshwar, a keynote speaker at PyCon US 2024, reflects on how her early positive experiences with open source and a pivotal sprint at PyCon North America 2015 inspired her to found Changed That, a consultancy focused on unblocking open source projects through targeted project management.
She shares an amusing anecdote about her initial reaction to receiving the keynote invitation, where she excitedly screamed in a library, inadvertently causing concern among staff and patrons.
Harihareshwar highlights the unique structure of PyCon US, which combines pre-accepted talks with the dynamic "Hatchery" program that allows attendees to organize their own sub-events, ensuring each conference offers a distinct array of sessions.
She also notes that 2024 marks the 20th anniversary of the popular web scraping tool BeautifulSoup, created by her spouse Leonard Richardson, who will be attending to celebrate the milestone.
Finally, she previews her keynote, which will blend behind-the-scenes stories from her work on Python package infrastructure like PyPI and pip with elements of standup comedy and theater to provide an engaging, in-person experience for the audience.
