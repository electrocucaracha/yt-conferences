# Summary

At PyCon US, conference chair Mariatta opened the day by acknowledging lower sponsorship numbers due to industry challenges while expressing gratitude for continued support.
She announced that the conference broke the Mastodon record for most posts on a single day and provided logistical updates, including swag pickup locations, Code of Conduct reminders, and details on the upcoming PyLadies auction.
Meta’s Dino Wieland then presented the company’s recent contributions to the Python ecosystem, highlighting advancements in PyTorch, the Pyre type checker, and the experimental free-threaded Python implementation available in version 3.13.
The session concluded with a Diversity and Inclusion panel featuring organizers from Ghana, Indonesia, Brazil, Texas, and Germany, who shared their personal journeys into community leadership, discussed the challenges of sustaining local groups, and emphasized the critical role of PSF grants and shared leadership in fostering inclusive global Python communities.
