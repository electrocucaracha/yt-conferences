# Summary

Mariatta, the PyCon US 2024 chair, delivered the final release notes, highlighting record-breaking attendance with 2,736 registrations and 1,752 first-time attendees, despite a slight decrease in sponsorship revenue to $918,967.
She emphasized the conference’s commitment to inclusivity and community support, noting that $351,436 in travel grants were awarded and the PyLadies auction raised a record $60,000, significantly exceeding previous years.
Mariatta expressed deep gratitude to sponsors, volunteers, and staff, including the human captioners and designers, before officially handing over the chair role to Elaine Wong for 2025.
Wong announced that PyCon US will return to Pittsburgh in May 2025 and revealed that the 2026 conference will be held in Long Beach, California, encouraging attendees to engage with regional Python conferences in the interim.
