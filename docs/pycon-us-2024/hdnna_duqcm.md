# Summary

Jay Miller, a keynote speaker at PyCon US 2024, attributes his successful transition from a non-developer to a developer advocate to the support and introductions provided by the San Diego Python community.
He highlights the growth of Black Python Devs, a global community he co-founded to foster togetherness and support for Black Pythonistas, which has expanded from a simple Discord server to an organization with 300 members that facilitates conference access and learning programs.
Miller shares a pivotal moment at DjangoCon US where developers from various African nations gathered in Durham, North Carolina, illustrating the strong desire for community connection among Black tech professionals.
He expresses immense excitement about delivering his keynote on the main stage, a goal he has held since learning Python through PyCon talks, and notes the warm encouragement he has received from mentors and peers.
Finally, Miller advises first-time attendees to pace themselves, stay hydrated, and actively seek out new connections by sitting at unfamiliar tables during lunch to build lasting professional relationships.
