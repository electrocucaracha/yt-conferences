# Summary

Cara Souls, Amelie Waser, and Anessa Pon introduce themselves as co-organizers of the Python Maintainers Summit at PyCon US 2024, highlighting their roles in supporting open source maintainers and the scientific Python ecosystem.
The summit, held on May 17th, marks its sixth year and serves as a full-day in-person event focused on discussing best practices for sustainable project development and community nurturing.
Key topics for discussion include technological tools and challenges, community growth and contributor retention, resource acquisition such as financial or legal support, and broader themes related to the joy and sustainability of open source maintenance.
The organizers emphasize the inclusive nature of the event, welcoming not only maintainers but also contributors, users, and those curious about open source, while noting that the call for proposals is open until March 25th with strong encouragement for first-time speakers and underrepresented groups.
Attendees are invited to register for the summit through the PyCon US platform, either during initial registration or by adding it to their existing activities, to join a vibrant community dedicated to sharing ideas and fostering collaboration.
