# Summary

Marata, the conference chair for PyCon US 2024, invites the Python community to join the event in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, from May 15 to 23, highlighting that it is the largest and longest-running Python conference in the world.
The conference features a diverse program including tutorials, keynotes, lightning talks, a job fair, and a sprint space for open-source contributions, with options to attend either in person or virtually.
To ensure accessibility, PyCon US offers a generous travel grants program, having distributed over $270,000 to more than 300 attendees in 2023, with applications opening in December and closing in February.
The call for proposals is currently open until December, encouraging students, hobbyists, and professionals to submit talks or tutorials, with speakers receiving priority for travel grants and tutorial presenters earning $1,500.
Additionally, the conference seeks volunteers for proposal review and speaker mentorship, as well as sponsors to support the event and the Python Software Foundation, while Marata shares her personal story of how financial aid and community support transformed her career.
