# Summary

Sumana Harihareswara, a former project manager for Python packaging, shares her experiences working on critical infrastructure like PyPI and pip, emphasizing that project management involves curiosity, stubbornness, and building trust to solve complex problems.
She recounts how she helped overhaul PyPI’s legacy codebase and improve pip’s dependency resolver by securing grants and coordinating volunteer efforts, illustrating how new kinds of help, such as UX research and grant writing, can transform stagnant open-source projects.
Harihareswara highlights the importance of clear communication and user experience, noting that even error messages serve as vital interfaces for users who rely on invisible infrastructure.
She also addresses the sustainability of volunteer work, urging contributors to regularly reassess their responsibilities to prevent burnout and ensure long-term project health.
Finally, she encourages the audience to renew old connections, intentionally manage their volunteer commitments, and recognize their ability to make a difference in large systems, whether in open source or local government, by acting as "defibrillators" that revive stuck processes.
