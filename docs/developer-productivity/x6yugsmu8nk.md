# Summary

The speaker, Emily, discusses the importance of technical leadership in teams, particularly in managing complexity and optimizing for learning in modern software development.
She argues that traditional scrum masters are not equipped to handle the complexities of code design and that tech leads need training and support to become effective technical coaches.
The speaker introduces her "Saman" method, which includes learning hours and Ensemble (team coding sessions), to help teams develop practical coding skills and manage complexity.
She emphasizes the need for democratizing technical coaching to make it more accessible to teams, rather than relying solely on external coaches or training programs.
