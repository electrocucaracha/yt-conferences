# Summary

As the Director of Engineering at Canva, Michelle shares her experiences working with distributed teams, lessons learned, and new experiments to adopt inclusive practices.
She emphasizes the importance of building an inclusive distributed team, where every person plays a role in making it successful, not just remote employees.
Michelle highlights the challenges of the "headquarters way" of thinking, which doesn't scale, and shares principles for building distributive teams, including asynchronous practices that reduce coupling and increase empowerment.
She also discusses the benefits of post-commit code review, transparency, and proactive written communication in distributed teams, as well as the importance of hiring t-shaped people who are capable of unblocking themselves.
