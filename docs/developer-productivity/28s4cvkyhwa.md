# Summary

Woody Zul, a software developer with 35 years of experience, shares his story on mob programming and its benefits.
Mob programming involves having all team members work together on the same task at the same time, sharing knowledge and skills to achieve better solutions and higher quality code.
By eliminating queues and excess inventory, teams can optimize for flow, achieving a state of high productivity and effectiveness, where individuals are able to focus on the right things and deliver meaningful results.
