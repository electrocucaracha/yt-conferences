# Summary

The speaker, Helen, discusses the challenges of onboarding engineers in a rapidly growing company like Spotify.
She shares her personal experience as an engineer who has been through internal mobility, having worked at the same company for six years.
To address this issue, Spotify created an internal developer portal called Backstage, which provides a single pane of glass for all technical documentation and tooling.
The platform includes features such as self-led guides, engineering boot camps, and software templates to streamline onboarding processes.
By empowering engineers with autonomy and providing tools like the skill exchange marketplace, Spotify aims to improve retention and productivity among its talent pool.
