# Summary

The speaker, Emily Rosengren, Director of Engineering at Granger, discusses the concept of "tech debt" and its limitations.
She argues that the traditional metaphor of tech debt as a static ledger is no longer relevant due to changes in software development, such as increased use of cloud-native systems and product-oriented teams.
Instead, she suggests reframing the conversation around technology to focus on the context of each project and the trade-offs made to achieve its goals.
Emily encourages engineers to stop labeling work as "tech debt" and instead explain why it matters, understand the product roadmap, and prioritize recommendations that align with product strategy.
By shifting the way they think about this work, engineers can influence the direction of products and get their recommendations prioritized.
