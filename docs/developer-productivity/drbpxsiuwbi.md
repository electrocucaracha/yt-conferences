# Summary

The speakers, Amon and Eamon, discuss the importance of learning from past mistakes in software development.
They highlight 10 lessons from the 1990s that are still relevant today, including the need to draw more diagrams, focus on fundamentals, and take responsibility for one's own work.
The speakers also emphasize the importance of testing, documentation, and mastering tools and shortcuts to improve productivity.
Additionally, they caution against complacency and remind developers to prepare for potential downturns in the industry by building a strong skillset and network.
