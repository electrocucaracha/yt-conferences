# Summary

Ken Kousen, a Java champion and author of "Help Your Boss Help You," discusses his book with Trisha Gee from the GOTO Book Club.
The book aims to help employees build a relationship with their boss that benefits both parties.
Kousen shares his experience as a manager and employee, highlighting the importance of communication, pushback, and empathy in managing conflicts.
He emphasizes that managers often lack training and may not be aware of the impact of their decisions on employees.
The book offers practical advice on how to navigate these situations and build a constructive relationship with one's boss.
Kousen also discusses the challenges of being an employee, particularly when it comes to maintaining friendships with colleagues while setting professional boundaries.
