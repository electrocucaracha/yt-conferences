# Summary

The speaker discusses the importance of experimentation in innovation, particularly in product development.
They share their personal experience of trying to write a book, but instead decided to test ideas through experiments such as creating a pre-order page and testing different marketing messages.
The speaker emphasizes the need for a growth mindset, acknowledging that uncertainty is high at the beginning of a project, and that experimentation is key to making informed decisions.
They also discuss the importance of measuring data, focusing on outcomes rather than requirements, and iterating quickly through an experiment backlog.
Ultimately, the speaker's goal is to help "turret builders" (product developers) succeed by embracing experimentation and iteration.
