# Summary

Roy Oshirov discusses the difference between waterfall pipelines and cooperative pipelines, which enable true continuous delivery.
He explains that many organizations struggle to achieve continuous delivery due to outdated rules and processes, but by adopting new technologies like pipelines, they can reduce bottlenecks and improve feedback cycles.
The key is to trust the pipeline and make it a part of the organization's decision-making process, rather than relying on manual approvals or testing.
This requires a cultural shift towards automation, continuous integration, and continuous delivery, as well as training and coaching for employees to adapt to new roles and responsibilities.
