# Summary

Scott Hanselman, a software professional, shares his approach to scaling himself and increasing productivity.
He emphasizes the importance of triaging tasks, focusing on effectiveness rather than efficiency, and eliminating distractions such as social media and email.
By prioritizing goals, using tools like the Pomodoro Technique, and delegating or deferring non-essential tasks, individuals can achieve more in less time and reduce feelings of overwhelm and guilt.
