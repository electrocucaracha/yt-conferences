# Summary

The speaker, a practitioner in chaos engineering, discusses building resilience with chaos engineering.
They emphasize that starting with a clear business case and understanding the customer's needs is crucial.
The speaker shares their experience with Amazon, where they introduced game days, simulated failure scenarios to test teams' ability to detect and resolve issues quickly.
They also discuss the importance of measuring trends, collecting data, and setting goals to drive improvement.
The speaker highlights the need for a culture shift, emphasizing that resilience is not extraordinary but an ordinary feature that can be learned and developed through behaviors, thoughts, and actions.
