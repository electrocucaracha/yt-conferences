# Summary

Avraham Poupko interviews Stefan Hofer and Henning Schwentner about their book "Domain Storytelling: A Collaborative, Visual, and Agile Way to Build Domain-Driven Software".
The authors explain that domain storytelling is a collaborative approach to understanding a domain and creating software that enables it.
They use storytelling as a way to capture the essence of the domain, rather than just listing requirements or specifications.
This approach allows for a more human-centered and empathetic understanding of the domain, which can lead to better software development outcomes.
The authors also discuss the challenges of translating this approach into written form, but emphasize that storytelling is an essential part of the process.
