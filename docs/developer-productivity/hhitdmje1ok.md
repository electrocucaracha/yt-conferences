# Summary

As an independent cloud architect and developer, Lin Lang shared her experience working with bioinformatics researchers in Australia to visualize their system.
She realized that visualizing systems is crucial for accessibility, scalability, and collaboration.
The team created a "hello world" example using Amazon's Terra platform, which enabled them to showcase their algorithm and collaborate with researchers worldwide.
Lin emphasized the importance of visualizing entire systems, avoiding bias, and reducing dimensionality to three to five aspects.
She also highlighted the need for reproducibility and usability across different domains.
