# Summary

Jeff Esel, a professional at Elastic, introduces himself and explains the purpose of his presentation.
He discusses the importance of search and gen in his work and mentions that he will be covering lexical 101, dense vector models, and semantic search.
Esel explains that dense vector models are used for most Vector search applications and output an array of floats, while sparse vectors are becoming more common and use a token and weight as output.
He also discusses the differences between dense and sparse vectors, including their output and usage in search.
