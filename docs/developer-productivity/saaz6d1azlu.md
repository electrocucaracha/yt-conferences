# Summary

The speaker, a software developer and author, discusses the importance of balancing feature development with structural design in software development.
They argue that traditional approaches to design, such as "design up front," can be ineffective due to economic realities, including survival, net present value, and optionality.
The speaker advocates for an interruptible style of design, where features and structure are developed in parallel, allowing for a balance between the two.
This approach enables designers to serve human relationships and economic realities simultaneously, leading to more sustainable software development.
