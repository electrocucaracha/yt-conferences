# Summary

The speaker discusses their approach to coding, focusing on making code easier to change.
They introduce the concept of "top-down programming," where a single sentence describes what needs to be done, and then break down that sentence into smaller parts.
This process helps identify state and leads to decoupling components.
The speaker also emphasizes the importance of intuition in design, suggesting that thinking about coding as building habits can make it more enjoyable and effective.
They advocate for simplicity, cohesion, and minimizing coupling, while encouraging experimentation and having fun with code.
