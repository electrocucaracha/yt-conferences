# Summary

Juli Nino, a Site Reliability Engineer at Adl Digital Labs, discusses the importance of combining chaos engineering with observability and resilience.
Chaos theory explains how systems can be both ordered and chaotic, and how non-linear systems can lead to failures.
Observability is crucial in understanding system behavior and learning from incidents.
The author shares seven attributes of observability: service health, telemetry, instrumentation, actions, traffic data, latency, and error rates.
Four key concepts related to resilience are also discussed: capability, factual, potential, and critical capabilities.
Juli Nino defines chaos engineering as the practice of experimenting with painless production failures to rebuild weaknesses and build confidence in resilience capabilities using observability and monitoring tools.
