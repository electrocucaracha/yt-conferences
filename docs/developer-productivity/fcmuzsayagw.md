# Summary

The speaker discusses the rapid pace of change in technology, citing Moore's Law, which states that transistors double approximately every two years.
This has led to a significant increase in computing power and opportunities for innovation.
The speaker shares their personal experience working with various technologies, including home computers and cloud-based services, highlighting how these advancements have enabled companies to deliver software more quickly and efficiently.
They also emphasize the importance of continuous learning and experimentation, as well as the need to adapt to changing circumstances.
The speaker argues that traditional approaches like waterfall and scrum can be limiting and that smaller teams working on individual problems can lead to greater autonomy and effectiveness.
