# Summary

In a game of Dungeons & Dragons, a balanced party consists of characters with different skills, backgrounds, and alignments.
Similarly, in software development, a well-rounded team requires diverse skill sets, expertise, and perspectives to tackle complex problems.
A project manager's role is to guide the team, provide direction, and facilitate communication among members.
However, it's essential to avoid knowledge silos, where one person hoards information, as this can lead to toxicity and decreased team performance.
By sharing knowledge, taking breaks, and reflecting on past experiences, teams can improve their processes, tools, and people to make future projects easier and more successful.
