# Summary

The speaker discusses the concept of "infobesity," which refers to the overwhelming amount of information available in today's digital age, leading to mental fatigue, lack of focus, anxiety, and decreased productivity.
They compare it to physical obesity, highlighting the need for prioritization and filtering of information to maintain a healthy balance.
The speaker shares their personal experience of overcoming infobesity through changes in habits, such as exercise, nutrition, and sleep, and how they applied similar principles to manage digital information consumption.
They also introduce tools like email filters, prioritization methods (e.g., the "method of information diet"), and a digital scale to measure progress.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of learning and acting on new knowledge, rather than just consuming it, and encourages the audience to take action towards improving their own infobesity.
