# Summary

Brendan, a GitLab employee, discusses the importance of knowing when to step away from work while working remotely.
He shares his experience of being part of an all-remote team for three years and highlights five main types of teams in remote work: no remote, remote allowed, hybrid remote, fully remote, and fully office-based.
Brendan emphasizes the need to separate work and personal life, building trust with employees, and creating a culture that values collaboration and community.
He also stresses the importance of defining values as leaders and relying on them to make decisions when faced with uncertainty.
