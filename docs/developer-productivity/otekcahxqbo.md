# Summary

Audun Strand, a fundamental principle engineer at NAV, Norway's largest government agency, discussed the challenges of developing software for the public sector.
He shared his experience with building an open-source platform called NAIS, which aims to make government software more accessible and maintainable.
Audun emphasized the importance of asynchronous communication, event-driven design, and loose coupling in achieving this goal.
He also highlighted the need for a "control without control" approach, balancing creative autonomy with large-scale efficiency alignment.
The conversation touched on topics such as data mesh, legacy code, and the superpower of software development, where developers can change software quickly and experiment with new ideas.
