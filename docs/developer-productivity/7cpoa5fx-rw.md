# Summary

Airbnb has implemented remote work policies, allowing employees to work from anywhere.
The company's experience with remote work has shown that while it presents challenges, such as communication and scheduling overhead, it can also improve efficiency and productivity.
To address these challenges, Airbnb has developed team norms and intentional culture strategies, including regular check-ins, asynchronous collaboration tools, and recognition and appreciation programs.
The company recognizes the importance of in-person elements for connection and teamwork, but acknowledges that remote work will continue to evolve and require ongoing adaptation.
By prioritizing trust, connection, engagement, and recognition, Airbnb aims to create effective remote engineering teams.
