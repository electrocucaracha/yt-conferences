# Summary

The speaker, a technical leader with experience in large and small companies, emphasizes the importance of innovation for organizations.
They share their personal journey from working on vacuum tube computers to writing the standard for today's most widely used programming language.
The speaker highlights the challenges of incremental development and the need to think ahead to invent the future.
They reference Alan Kay's concept of playing "Wayne Gretzky's game," where one anticipates where technology is headed, rather than just following it.
The speaker explains Kay's 8-step approach to inventing the future, which involves identifying favorable exponentials, backtracking halfway, and prototyping a midpoint solution.
They share examples of how this approach was applied in the development of the Dynabook and Tesla's electric cars, and provide a tool for organizations to encourage long-term thinking and innovation.
