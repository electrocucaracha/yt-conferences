# Summary

The speaker discusses various topics, including the evolution of technology, platform engineering, data mesh, generative science, and how work gets done.
They argue that software engineers are not yet truly engineers, citing examples such as the Curiosity Rover's landing on Mars, which required a team of experts to overcome numerous challenges.
The speaker also talks about the importance of continuous delivery, building microservices, and evolutionary architectures.
Additionally, they discuss the concept of batch size reduction, which can enable teams to shorten their cycle time by an order of magnitude or more without changing capacity or utilization.
The speaker references various books and authors, including Martin Fowler, Dan North, and Don Riesner, and shares a prototype that simulates how work gets done using behavior trees and agents.
