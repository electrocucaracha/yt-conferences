# Summary

The speaker emphasizes the importance of effective communication in presentation skills, particularly for technical subjects.
They suggest that to improve, one must talk about topics they know and love, tell a story, and structure their content around a narrative flow.
The speaker also stresses the need for minimalistic slides with clear headings, concise text, and relevant visuals.
Additionally, they advise on how to handle questions from the audience, including deferring answers when unsure or unable to provide information, and using this opportunity to engage with the audience.
By applying these strategies, presenters can improve their communication skills, relax their audience, and make their presentations more engaging and effective.
