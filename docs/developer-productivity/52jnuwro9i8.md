# Summary

The speaker reflects on the evolution of technology and programming over the years, from his early days with Solaris in the late 1990s to the current era of cloud computing and managed languages.
He notes that while some aspects have improved, such as access to open-source software and community support, others have become more complex and stressful, like remote work during the pandemic.
The speaker believes that as one stays in the industry long enough, they naturally gravitate towards philosophical thinking and distilling complex concepts down to their most fundamental principles.
He humorously acknowledges his own limitations, saying he needs a good teacher to learn how to code, but is grateful for the opportunities he's had and the chance to share his experiences with others.
