# Summary

The speaker discusses the prerequisites for chaos engineering, a practice used to improve system reliability.
They dispel myths that chaos engineering requires expertise or is only suitable for large companies like Netflix.
Instead, they emphasize the importance of instrumentation, social awareness, well-defined hypotheses, and alignment among team members to conduct experiments safely and effectively.
The speaker also shares early research data from Varica, which found that many organizations are adopting chaos engineering practices, with 83% hoping to start a program this year, despite not being "wizard level" experts.
