# Summary

Kevlin Henney is joined by Hadi Hariri from JetBrains to discuss developer advocacy, complexity, and the evolution of software development.
Hadi shares his experiences working on various projects, including a funeral home software, where he realized that over-engineering can lead to unnecessary complexity.
He also discusses the importance of understanding context and not assuming that someone else's problem is your own.
The conversation touches on topics such as design patterns, microservices, and the challenges of creating simple yet effective solutions.
Hadi emphasizes the need for balance in software development, where abstraction and benefits are desirable but should not come at the cost of clarity and comprehension.
