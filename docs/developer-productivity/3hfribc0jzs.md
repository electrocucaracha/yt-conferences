# Summary

A software engineer, Rose, was working on a task when her colleague interrupted her, causing her to abandon the task.
She then had to troubleshoot an issue that took an hour to resolve.
After returning to her task, she realized that the requirements were unclear, and she couldn't make progress without involving another team member who wasn't available.
To overcome this, Rose went back to reviewing code changes she had sent out two days earlier.
However, when she tried to commit her work, a test failed due to a flaky test.
She investigated further and found that the issue was with the testing process, not her code.
Despite facing obstacles, including interruptions, system failures, unclear requirements, long response times, slow code turnaround times, and flaky tests, Rose persevered and eventually made progress on her task.
