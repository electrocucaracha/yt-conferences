# Summary

Gojko Gogichaichvili, a prolific author and software developer, discusses his new book "Lizard Optimization" with Dave Farley from the GOTO Book Club.
The book focuses on finding and addressing edge cases in product development to improve overall user experience.
Gojko shares examples of how he used this approach to optimize his products, such as Narakeet and MindMup, by identifying and addressing unusual usage patterns, like users creating blank videos or running software on fridges.
He emphasizes the importance of understanding "lizard behaviors" - unusual but statistically significant user behavior - to create better products for everyone, not just those who are misusing them.
