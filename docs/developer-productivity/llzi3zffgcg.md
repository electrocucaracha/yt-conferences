# Summary

Adam Tornhill, founder of Empear, discussed prioritizing technical depth in software development, emphasizing organizational and social factors over code properties.
He noted that as teams grow, coordination overhead increases, leading to motivation losses.
Tornhill advocates for small teams (3-4 people) and autonomous teams to scale, aligning with business structure.
He also highlighted the importance of facilitation, psychological safety, and research on team effectiveness, citing studies like Google's Project Aristotle, which demonstrate that mid-level or junior engineers can be more effective than senior engineers with low psychological safety.
