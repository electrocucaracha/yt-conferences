# Summary

The speakers, Farron Wallace and Benjamin Mitchell, discuss the importance of understanding psychological biases in software development.
They acknowledge that humans are prone to biases, which can impact team dynamics and project outcomes.
To overcome these biases, they suggest creating a culture where it's safe to have open conversations about differences in opinion, using facilitation techniques to balance individual perspectives, and fostering strong relationships with colleagues.
By acknowledging and addressing biases, teams can work more effectively together, leading to better software development outcomes.
