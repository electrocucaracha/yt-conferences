# Summary

The speaker shares their experience of struggling with code reviews during their first few months at Shopify, where they received harsh feedback on their work.
They realized that great code reviews are both a symptom and a contributing cause to highly effective teams.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of breaking down large pieces of work into smaller, manageable chunks, making it easier for reviewers to provide actionable feedback.
They also stress the need for open-mindedness, labeling PRs properly, and selecting the right people for review.
By implementing these strategies, teams can move faster, build better things, and create a culture of continuous improvement.
