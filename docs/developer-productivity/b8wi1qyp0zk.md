# Summary

The three authors, Evelyn van Kelle, Gien Verschatse, and Kenny Baas-Schwegler, discuss their collaborative approach to software design in their book "Collaborative Software Design: How to Facilitate Domain Modeling Decisions." They share their personal backgrounds and how they transitioned into the field of collaborative modeling.
The authors emphasize the importance of understanding human behavior and social dynamics in software development, highlighting that technology is not the only factor at play.
They also discuss the concept of "shadows" - unconscious biases and experiences that can impact group dynamics - and how to address them through collaboration and facilitation.
The authors recommend various resources, including books and podcasts, for those interested in learning more about collaborative modeling and decision-making.
