# Summary

Jodie Virtual and Michelle Frost, AI advocates at Jet Brains, discuss the evolution of conversations about AI, from its early days to the current state of generative AI models.
They highlight the importance of understanding the differences between artificial narrow intelligence (ANI) and artificial general intelligence (AGI), with AGI being a well-defined concept that is still lacking in definition.
The conversation also touches on the influence of science fiction on modern AI development, with some researchers holding transhumanist or doomer beliefs, and the need for pragmatism and realism in the field.
Additionally, they discuss the benefits of LLMs in developer productivity, particularly in greenfield projects, but caution against over-reliance on these tools for solving complex business problems.
