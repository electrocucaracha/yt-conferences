# Summary

Kevlin Henney discusses the importance of simplicity in software development, citing his experience as CTO of an eCommerce company where he implemented a more monadic and functional coding style, resulting in shorter and more robust code.
He emphasizes the need to ask questions about what problems are being solved and how they can be simplified, rather than just adding complexity for the sake of it.
Henney also highlights the importance of questioning one's own assumptions and mental models, and encourages developers to adopt a more inquiry-based approach to their work.
