# Summary

The speaker discusses the durability of certain ideas in software development, which will likely remain relevant in 100 years.
They argue that iteration, experimentation, feedback, and empirical discovery are essential for effective software development, as they are humanity's best problem-solving techniques.
The scientific method, applied to software development, enables a more experimental approach to creating software, with continuous delivery being a key aspect of this discipline.
Modularity, information hiding, separation of concerns, abstraction, and cohesion will also remain crucial in managing complexity and building complex systems.
