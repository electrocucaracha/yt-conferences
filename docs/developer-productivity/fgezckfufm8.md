# Summary

The discussion revolves around developer productivity, measuring it, and its impact on organizations.
The panelists emphasize that productivity is not a developer individual characteristic but rather a characteristic of the system of work.
They argue that traditional metrics such as activity levels are flawed and that more nuanced approaches are needed to understand what drives productivity.
One panelist suggests using value stream mapping to measure the work going through the system, while another proposes using surveys to gather perceptions of developer experience.
The conversation also touches on the future of AI in software development, with some panelists expressing skepticism about its potential impact on jobs, while others see it as a tool that can augment human capabilities and improve productivity.
