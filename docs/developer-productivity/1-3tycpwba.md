# Summary

The speaker, John LeDrew, discusses the five pillars of collaborative product ownership.
He emphasizes the importance of respecting people, not bringing solutions, and showing that you respect their ability to solve problems.
The pillars are: (1) Respect people, (2) Don't bring your team's solutions, (3) Show the team that you actually respect their ability to solve problems and bring them problems to solve because that's what they do.
