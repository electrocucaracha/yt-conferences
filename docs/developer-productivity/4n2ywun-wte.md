# Summary

Here is a summary of the video transcript in 3-5 sentences: Platform engineering is a discipline that involves building, maintaining, and providing a curated platform experience for various communities, including technical teams, data analysts, scientists, leadership, and governance.
A successful platform experience requires clear boundaries and responsibilities, self-service capabilities, flexible and evolvable technology and processes, reliability, and flexibility in terms of choice and customization.
The platform engineering team should aim to be a role model and walk the talk by using their own tools and reference examples, offering professional services to teams, and respecting and recognizing differences among communities.
