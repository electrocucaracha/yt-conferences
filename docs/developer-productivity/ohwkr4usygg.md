# Summary

The speaker discusses chaos engineering, a process of stressing an application in test or production environment to improve its resilience.
They share their personal experience with outages and how they learned from them, including the importance of intuition and awareness in surviving outages.
The speaker also talks about fighting biases, such as confirmation bias and sunk cost fallacy, and how to remove friction during training to make chaos engineering more accessible.
They introduce the Fault Injection Simulator, a fully managed service that aims to simplify the process of getting started with chaos engineering.
