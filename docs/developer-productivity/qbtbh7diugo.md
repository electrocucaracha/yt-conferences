# Summary

Dave Thomas, co-founder of The Pragmatic Programmer, discusses his new book Simplicity, which focuses on individual developers' ability to create simplicity in their work.
He emphasizes that simplicity is not easy to achieve and requires mastery of a skill or discipline.
Thomas recommends using an "Orient-Step-Learn" process to gather feedback and improve one's work, starting with small changes such as keeping a daybook to track progress and identifying areas where dependencies can be reduced.
He also stresses the importance of empathy and transparency in personal interactions, and encourages readers to recognize their own agency in creating positive change.
