# Summary

The speaker, Yan, discusses the importance of simplicity in technology development.
He shares his experience working on game engines at EA and how he eventually left to build his own engine, Hazel, which has a simpler architecture than existing solutions like Unity.
Yan argues that simplicity allows for faster development, better performance, and more flexibility, as it enables developers to focus on the core problem without getting bogged down in unnecessary complexity.
He also emphasizes the importance of making Simplicity Central to one's design and encourages developers to consider custom solutions but not at the expense of simplicity.
