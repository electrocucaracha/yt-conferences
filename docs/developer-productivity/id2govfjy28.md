# Summary

Low-code solutions aim to simplify software development, but their limitations and risks should not be overlooked.
While they can be useful in limited, constrained problem domains, they often ignore fundamental complexities of software development, such as version control, testing, and error handling.
These tools can lead to overconfidence among users, who may underestimate the difficulties of software development and overplay the power of low-code systems.
Professional developers need to recognize these limitations and continue to develop fundamental skills, including learning, experimentation, and incremental progress, to effectively use low-code solutions.
