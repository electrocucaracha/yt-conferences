# Summary

Bernd, co-founder of Carmona, discusses the importance of process automation, which involves modeling and executing sequences of tasks to achieve specific business outcomes.
He emphasizes that most developers think of process automation as a "business thingy," but it's actually about solving complex problems like waiting for asynchronous messages or dealing with long-running tasks.
Bernd argues that using workflow engines can help break down siloed metrics and improve collaboration between teams, and provides guidance on getting started with process automation, including choosing the right tool and metrics to track success.
