# Summary

The speaker discusses personal productivity, particularly for developers who often face overwhelming tasks and responsibilities after being promoted.
They introduce Stephen Covey's quadrant system, which helps prioritize tasks based on importance and urgency.
The key takeaway is to create a daily list of tasks in order of priority, rather than by time, and to regularly review and improve this process through "retrospective" reflection.
By doing so, developers can focus on the most important tasks first and avoid getting sidetracked by less critical activities.
