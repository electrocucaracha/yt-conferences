# Summary

The speaker discusses progressive delivery, a concept that builds on continuous delivery and feature flags.
Progressive delivery involves gradually rolling out new versions of software to a subset of users, allowing for testing and learning before scaling up to the entire user base.
The goal is to avoid downtime, limit the "blast radius" of errors, and enable teams to make data-driven decisions about their releases.
By automating guardrails and using feature flags, teams can focus on what works and get a sense of dopamine feedback, rather than being stuck in a death march of building something that never goes anywhere.
