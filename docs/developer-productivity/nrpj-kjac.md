# Summary

Laura Tacho, CTO at DX, discussed her role and experiences as a technical leader.
She emphasized the importance of being a business leader, not just an engineering leader, and having a clear understanding of how decisions impact the organization's bottom line.
The Core Four framework, which she developed with Abi Noda, unifies various frameworks for measuring developer productivity, including DORA, SPACE, and DevEx.
It emphasizes speed, effectiveness, quality, and impact, with key metrics for each category.
Laura also discussed the challenges of introducing metrics in organizations, such as avoiding gamification and ensuring transparency, and offered recommendations for managing team performance, including skip-level check-ins and public performance management.
