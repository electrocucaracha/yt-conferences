# Summary

The speaker discusses software design, emphasizing its importance in creating powerful systems.
They introduce the concept of "participatory sense-making," where teams collaborate to create a shared understanding of their software's capabilities and limitations.
The speaker uses the example of a fantastical creature company, where team members work together to design a new creature, incorporating feedback from each other and customers.
They highlight the importance of bounded contexts, which are specific languages used within different domains, allowing for precise communication and reducing cognitive load.
The speaker also emphasizes the need for observability in software development, enabling teams to understand their product's behavior and make data-driven decisions.
Finally, they offer practical advice on how to start implementing these concepts, including getting precise with language and embracing diverse perspectives.
