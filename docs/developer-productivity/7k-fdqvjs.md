# Summary

The speaker, Kat, discusses the importance of understanding why metrics are being measured in software development.
She argues that if a team doesn't know why they're measuring something, it's likely to be a bad idea.
Kat shares her experience with a team at Verica, where she intentionally made slides look ugly to test people's memory for painful or unpleasant things.
She introduces the concept of leading versus lagging indicators, explaining that lagging indicators like time and process are useful but can become unreliable if work is piling up.
Instead, teams should focus on leading indicators like the difference between arrival and departure rates.
Kat offers actionable tips, such as using minimal labeling on charts to draw attention to trends rather than absolute numbers, avoiding red and green color schemes that may be perceived as judgmental, and focusing on reducing waiting time in software development.
