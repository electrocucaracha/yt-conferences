# Summary

Speaking at Agent Conf North America, a representative from the PyTorch Foundation highlighted the rapid growth of the AI community and the critical need to move beyond simply pointing agents at large models.
The speaker emphasized that achieving optimal performance, cost efficiency, and value requires integrating feedback loops directly into the intelligence layer, a theme reinforced by recent sessions at the PyTorch conference.
A key trend discussed is the shift away from brute-force scaling of larger models toward optimizing smaller, specialized models for specific workloads, which can outperform frontier models in both accuracy and cost.
This approach is part of a new era of "on-the-job training," where models are continuously retrained using real-world outcomes rather than being treated as static, finished products.
The speaker cited examples from Uber and Shopify, noting that Shopify’s continual learning loop allows them to build specialist models that surpass frontier models in efficiency and results, thereby demonstrating the power of open-source frameworks in driving better business outcomes through dynamic, iterative improvement.
