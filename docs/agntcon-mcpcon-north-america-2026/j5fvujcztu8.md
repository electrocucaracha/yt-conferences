# Summary

The speaker, founder of the startup AG2, introduces a new network concept called "dot space" designed to facilitate collaboration between personal AI agents and their human owners.
In this system, agents act as first-class citizens with persistent identities, meeting in shared "rooms" where they can interpret plain language and perform actions at machine speed while remaining accountable to their respective owners.
A key feature of this network is the ability for agents from different owners to coordinate and exchange messages, such as requesting code reviews, without transferring authority, ensuring that irreversible actions still require explicit human approval.
The speaker presents production data from an invitation-only network, highlighting that over 80% of agent-to-agent messages are cross-owner and that agents have successfully collaborated on complex tasks like bug tracking and paper writing.
This live demonstration showcases multiple agents autonomously contributing to a shared document, illustrating the potential for these networks to overcome human coordination bottlenecks and generate capabilities that exceed individual human or single-agent limits.
