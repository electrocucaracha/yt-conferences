# Summary

Ian, a founder at Proof, introduces the company’s mission to secure internet transactions using legal identity and cryptographic credentials.
He explains that Proof has spent a decade digitizing high-stakes processes like mortgages and notarizations, and now applies this technology to the emerging field of agentic AI.
The core innovation is "401," an open standard developed with partners like Google, Visa, and Circle, which allows sites to verify the human identity behind an AI agent via HTTP headers.
This protocol enables secure actions such as opening bank accounts, making payments, and signing legal documents by presenting verifiable credentials from digital wallets.
Ian demonstrates live workflows where agents interact with services like Carrot and Bank of America, using biometric verification and PKI signatures to establish legal reliance and prevent fraud.
He emphasizes that unlike generic API calls, Proof’s system provides a root of trust that can withstand legal disputes, effectively bridging the gap between AI automation and human accountability in financial and legal ecosystems.
