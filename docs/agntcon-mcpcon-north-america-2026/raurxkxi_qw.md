# Summary

JR from the Linux Foundation introduces the Tokenomics Foundation, a new initiative focused on defining "AI economics" by tracing the path from energy and capital inputs through hardware to measurable business value.
He argues that traditional metrics like token counts or cost per token are insufficient, advocating instead for a holistic view of total AI costs that includes storage, networking, licensing, and labor impacts.
The presentation highlights a critical disconnect where organizations often lack the ability to demonstrate clear ROI to financial stakeholders, risking a repeat of past cloud repatriation cycles due to unmanaged expenses.
To address this, the foundation is developing tools and standards across the entire AI supply chain, from data center production to efficient consumption and value realization, ensuring that AI investments translate into tangible outcomes like revenue growth and cost avoidance.
JR concludes by inviting the audience to explore the "State of Tokenomics" survey and attend the upcoming conference in San Diego to further engage with the community building these economic frameworks.
