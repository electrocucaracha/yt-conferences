# Summary

Vultr, the world’s largest private independently held cloud infrastructure platform, presents a new era of enterprise infrastructure designed to support the transition from model training to the adoption of agentic AI.
The company argues that the traditional focus on massive, centralized superclusters for training is ill-suited for inference, advocating instead for a distributed, composable cloud stack that leverages 34 global data center regions across six continents.
This approach allows enterprises to maintain data sovereignty, ensure low latency, and integrate AI-native applications using existing cloud-native principles such as DevOps and platform engineering.
By offering self-service, API-first tools and one-click clusters for both CPUs and GPUs, Vultr enables organizations to scale efficiently without vendor lock-in, while prioritizing security, compliance, and cost-effectiveness.
Ultimately, the presentation emphasizes that the future of AI infrastructure lies in distributed, secure platforms that deliver personalized, high-performance outcomes while keeping proprietary data under strict control.
