# Summary

Sarah Evans and Crob present a lighthearted overview of the agentic AI supply chain, using a custom "AI Candy Land" board game to illustrate the various personas and security challenges involved in developing these systems.
The talk highlights how traditional DevSecOps models are insufficient for AI, necessitating new approaches that integrate roles such as data scientists, product managers, and AI engineers into the secure development lifecycle.
Key OpenSSF resources, including the Salsa specification, Sigstore for model signing, and the Acritias project for vulnerability disclosure, are introduced as essential tools for securing dependencies, ensuring provenance, and managing the surge in AI-generated vulnerability reports.
The speakers emphasize the critical importance of understanding regulatory compliance, such as the EU Cyber Resilience Act, and the need for cross-functional collaboration to address the unique risks posed by large language models and agentic infrastructure.
Finally, they invite the audience to engage with the OpenSSF community and attend a technical workshop on the Secure Agentic Framework, which maps security controls to MITRE ATT&CK techniques to help organizations navigate governance, risk, and compliance hurdles in agentic AI deployments.
