# Summary

Paul, the founder of Gamgee, shares his journey of creating the world’s first computationally designed mRNA canine cancer vaccine for his dog, Rosie, after she was misdiagnosed and later developed cancer.
Leveraging his background in machine learning and extensive use of AI to navigate complex genomics and regulatory landscapes, Paul compared cancer and baseline DNA to identify specific mutations that made the cells invisible to the immune system.
By combining DNA sequencing, computational genomics, and mRNA technology, he developed a custom vaccine construct that effectively retrained Rosie’s immune system to recognize and attack the cancer.
The treatment resulted in a 50% to 75% reduction in tumor size and unprecedented immune system infiltration, demonstrating the potential of AI-driven personalized medicine.
Paul encourages others to tackle interdisciplinary problems by using AI to build understanding, find collaborators, and rigorously test their solutions to drive real-world impact.
