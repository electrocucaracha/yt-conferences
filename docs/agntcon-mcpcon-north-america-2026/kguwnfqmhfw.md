# Summary

At the San Jose conference, the Agentic AI Foundation announced the addition of four major open-source projects to its stack, including Open Sandbox from Alibaba, Open Responses from OpenAI, Agent Skills from Anthropic, and Open AMF from Hugging Face.
The foundation also introduced three new governing committees focused on end user groups, applied industries, and startups to better serve its diverse community.
Highlighting rapid growth, the organization reported reaching 305 members in just ten months, making it the fastest-growing foundation in Linux Foundation history, while welcoming five new gold members including Vercel, Nutanix, Volterra, Ant Group, and MongoDB.
Recent surveys indicate that 81% of members have already deployed agentic AI and 89% have implemented MCP in production, signaling a significant industry shift toward open standards.
The speaker concluded by urging the community to actively participate in this historical moment by contributing code, designs, and industry knowledge to shape the future of the open agentic AI stack.
