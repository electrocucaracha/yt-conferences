# Summary

The Agent Conf MCP North America conference welcomes over 4,200 attendees from more than 1,500 companies, marking its largest event to date with a program featuring over 200 speakers sharing production experiences with AI agents.
The organizers express gratitude to their diamond, platinum, gold, and silver sponsors, including major tech firms like AWS, Google Cloud, and OpenAI, for making the event possible.
Attendees are encouraged to explore the solution showcase, which offers a demo theater, poster sessions, arcade games, and a project pavilion for meeting maintainers of key agent frameworks.
Additionally, the conference highlights the recent launch of the first official, vendor-neutral MCP certification, developed in collaboration with industry leaders, allowing participants to enroll and become MCP Associates.
