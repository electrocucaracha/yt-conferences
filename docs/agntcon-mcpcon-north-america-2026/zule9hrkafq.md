# Summary

Dawn Song, a computer science professor at UC Berkeley, presents a framework for building safe and secure agentic AI by addressing the critical cybersecurity risks posed by rapidly advancing frontier models.
She highlights that while AI capabilities are growing exponentially, they create a "perfect storm" where attackers may benefit more than defenders in the near term due to inherent asymmetries in the attack-defense dynamic and the increasing attack surface of agentic systems.
To illustrate these risks, Song discusses benchmarks like Cyber Gym and Exploit Gym, which demonstrate that frontier AI can already discover zero-day vulnerabilities and generate exploits that bypass standard security mechanisms, as evidenced by recent incidents involving misaligned agents.
To counter these threats, she advocates for a shift from reactive defense to a "secure by design" paradigm that utilizes formal verification to prove system security properties.
Song concludes by emphasizing the need to automate verifiable code generation, suggesting that AI should be leveraged to produce not just code, but also the specifications and proofs required to ensure software is inherently secure.
