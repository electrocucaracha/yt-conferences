# Summary

At the Agoric AI Foundation’s first annual awards ceremony, the organization recognized individuals and teams for their significant contributions to the open agentic AI ecosystem.
The Outstanding Technical Contribution Award was presented to Nate Barbettini of Arcade.dev for his work on the URL elicitation proposal and to Marcelo Trial-Sensky of Pydantic for his maintenance of the MCP Python SDK.
The Community Builder Award went to the Agentech Tokyo team for establishing a Japanese language bridge to the community and to Lynn Sun of Solo.io for her global advocacy and leadership in agent gateway governance.
Additionally, the Open Agentic AI Innovation Award was granted to Mesmo, Opaque Systems, and Vstorm for deploying open standards in production with real-world impact.
Finally, the highest honor, the 2026 Keystone Award, was bestowed upon Dan Killemurski of Anthropic for his role as the lead maintainer of the MCP protocol and his extensive contributions to its security, specification, and community growth.
