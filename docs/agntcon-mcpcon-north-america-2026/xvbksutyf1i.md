# Summary

Manic, CTO and co-founder of the Agentic AI Foundation, argues that the critical infrastructure for AI agents—referred to as "plumbing"—requires neutral governance to prevent single-vendor capture.
He explains that while open-source licenses are necessary, they are insufficient because companies can revoke openness at any time; therefore, a neutral foundation is essential to permanently secure protocols for identity, payments, and tool usage.
The speaker highlights the urgent risk that agentic commerce could be controlled by a single entity, which would allow them to capture purchase decisions rather than just taking a transaction fee, a scenario he deems more dangerous than traditional toll-booth models.
Citing the foundation’s rapid growth to 300 member companies and active working groups, he emphasizes that the industry is currently in a critical window where these standards are being defined.
He concludes by urging the audience to demand open protocols, contribute to the commons, and build in the open to ensure the agentic era remains a shared public utility rather than a private walled garden.
