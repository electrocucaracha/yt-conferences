# Summary

In this video, Doug Howard emphasizes the importance of providing constructive feedback to team members, particularly for engineering leaders.
He outlines twelve significant consequences of avoiding negative feedback, including missed learning opportunities, stagnant individual growth, decreased employee engagement, increased turnover, diminished team dynamics, reduced productivity, lack of accountability, negative impact on organizational culture, misalignment with goals, missed innovation, weakened relationships, and decreased customer satisfaction.
Doug explains that withholding feedback leads to disengagement, inefficiency, and a decline in both team morale and performance.
He encourages managers to embrace the discomfort of giving feedback, as it is essential for professional growth, team cohesion, and organizational success.
Doug concludes by offering resources to help leaders become more effective at delivering constructive feedback.
