# Summary

The speaker introduces the concept of zines—small, DIY booklets often found in coffee shops or bookstores—and shares their personal experiences creating and using them, particularly for note-taking and troubleshooting in technical contexts like Kubernetes.

The session concludes with thanks and an invitation to explore zine-making further.
