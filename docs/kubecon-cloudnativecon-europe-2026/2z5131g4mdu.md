# Summary

The speaker, a software architect at Echko, discusses the persistent issue of unpatched vulnerabilities (CVEs) in container images, based on a year-long study scanning hundreds of popular open-source container projects.
Their research tracked the journey of over 40,000 CVEs and found that while 90% of vulnerabilities have available fixes, about 40% remain present in containers for more than six months, often due to delays in updating images or users not pulling fixed versions.
The talk highlights challenges such as the overwhelming volume of low-quality, AI-generated vulnerability reports that burden maintainers, the lack of resources in many open-source projects, and the complexity of dependency management.
Practical recommendations include using minimal base images to reduce unnecessary packages, leveraging tools like Dependabot and Renovate for dependency updates, and considering patching tools like Copacetic for urgent fixes.
Ultimately, the main bottleneck is not the creation of fixes but the slow adoption and propagation of those fixes into production containers.
