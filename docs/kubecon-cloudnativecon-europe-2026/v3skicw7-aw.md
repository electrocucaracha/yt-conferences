# Summary

Chris Plank, a CNCF platform engineering community leader and UK bank employee, presents a lighthearted overview of the evolution from physical data transfer methods, such as floppy disks and CDs, to modern cloud-based practices like GitOps.
He traces the journey through increasing network connectivity, the rise of DevOps, and the adoption of tools like Git, Jenkins, Docker, Kubernetes, and Helm, highlighting how these advancements shifted responsibilities toward developers and enabled faster, more automated deployments.
Plank explains that Kubernetes controllers and custom resource definitions (CRDs) introduced a declarative, controller-driven model, allowing for continuous reconciliation of desired states in clusters.
He emphasizes the significance of GitOps as a pull-based model, with popular tools like Flux and Argo automating deployment and management processes.
Plank concludes by recommending free Linux Foundation training resources and FluxCon talks for those interested in learning more about GitOps.
