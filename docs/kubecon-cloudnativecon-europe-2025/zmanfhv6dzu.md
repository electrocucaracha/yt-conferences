# Summary

The Kubernetes steering committee is a governing body responsible for decision-making and oversight in the project.
The committee consists of six members, including Mache, Stephen, Paco, and four other friends.
They oversee various aspects of the project, such as release planning, technical direction, and community engagement.
The committee also has several sub-committees, including SIG release, SIG apps, and SIG docs, which focus on specific areas of the project.
These groups work together to ensure the smooth operation of Kubernetes, with a focus on community involvement and collaboration.
