# Summary

The speaker discusses the threat of quantum computers to current cryptographic systems, particularly asymmetric cryptography, which could enable "side-channel attacks" where attackers can capture encrypted data now and decrypt it later with a future quantum computer.
To mitigate this, post-quantum secure key exchange mechanisms are critical, such as hybrid algorithms like Kyber X2559.
The speaker notes that while these algorithms may have higher computational costs, the cost of TLS handshakes in most deployments is not a bottleneck.
They encourage end-users to upgrade their software when new versions become available and to track vendors' offerings for post-quantum cryptography capabilities.
