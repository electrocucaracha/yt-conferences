# Summary

Vadim Mau, co-maintainer of Project Harour, discusses their experience with the Alfix mentorship program, which connects mentors and mentees to work on a project under guidance and supervision.
Over two and a half years, they've had 12 mentees, resulting in successful outcomes such as new project maintainers, employment opportunities, and created projects.
The program's success is attributed to its ability to filter out unqualified applicants through engagement and commitment criteria, reducing the need for extensive CV reviews.
