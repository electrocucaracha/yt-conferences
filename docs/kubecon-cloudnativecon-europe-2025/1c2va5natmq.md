# Summary

Holly Cumins, a professional, discusses the "zombie problem" of underutilized servers that consume resources without delivering value.
She cites research showing that up to 30% of servers are idle for six months or more, and that this can lead to significant cost savings and environmental benefits by optimizing server utilization.
To address this issue, Cumins advocates for a "light switch ops" approach, which involves automating the process of turning servers on and off, using tools like Kubernetes and cloud-native technologies, and implementing a culture of elasticity and desification.
