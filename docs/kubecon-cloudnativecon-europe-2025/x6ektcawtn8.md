# Summary

Nicole Vanderhovven, a senior developer advocate at Graphfana Labs, discusses her skepticism about AI and its limitations.
She references Isaac Asimov's three laws of robotics, which she believes are flawed because they don't account for observability.
Asimov's story "Runaround" illustrates the problem when a robot gets stuck in a loop due to conflicting instructions.
Vanderhovven proposes the addition of a zeroth law, making robots observable, to ensure that AI systems can be trusted and their performance can be evaluated.
She also highlights the importance of testing AI systems using tools like K6, which allows for end-to-end testing and evaluation of AI models.
