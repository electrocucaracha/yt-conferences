# Summary

Phil and Abby welcome to "Working Code Wins" at CubeCon, where they'll show how technology can transform hackathons.
Phil is a professional with experience in internal tooling and community engagement, while Abby has worked on platform engineering and Kubernetes.
They'll demonstrate how to use technology to create a platform service using Kratics, an open-source platform-as-a-service (PaaS) framework.
The workshop will cover the basics of PaaS, including creating a promise, managing resources, and deploying applications.
Phil will share his experience with hackathons and how he learned Kubernetes and platform engineering in just one weekend.
