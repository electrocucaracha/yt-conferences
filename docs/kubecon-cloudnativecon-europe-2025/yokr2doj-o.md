# Summary

The CNCF Technical Advisory Group (TAG) is a collaborative effort between the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) and its technical community to provide guidance on cloud-native projects.
The current structure consists of five tags: workloads foundation, runtime, infrastructure, operational resiliency, and security and compliance.
These tags will be restructured in the near future, with sub-projects and initiatives emerging from existing working groups, such as container orchestrated devices and IoT edge.
The goal is to enable community growth and facilitate collaboration among projects, while maintaining a flexible structure that allows for easy participation and feedback.
