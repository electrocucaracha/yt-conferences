# Summary

The speaker, Takashin Matsu from Hitachi Limited Japan, presented a two-part talk on recent updates in Keycloak, a popular identity and access management platform.
The first part focused on security future support, introducing the Tro project's special interest groups and community activities aimed at making Keycloak more secure.
He discussed two key security specification updates: RFC 9449 O2 (DOP) for verifiable credentials issuance and OID4 VCI to prevent misuse of stolen access tokens.
The second part was presented by Ryan Emerson from Red Hat, who introduced enhancements to Keycloak's observability story, including improved documentation, tracing capabilities, and logging features.
He demonstrated the new troubleshooting dashboard and showed how it can help identify issues in real-time, providing a valuable tool for users to monitor and optimize their Keycloak deployments.
