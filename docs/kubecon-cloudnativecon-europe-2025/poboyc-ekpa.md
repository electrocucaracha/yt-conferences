# Summary

The ISTO project has made significant progress in its history, from its initial goal of making Istio transparent to application workload management.
The project's adoption and growth have been driven by the community, with Google playing a crucial role in driving it towards CNCF membership in 2022.
To address this goal, ISTO introduced STO (Service Topology Object) in ambient mode, which provides a two-layer architecture for layer 4 traffic handling and mutual TLS simple authorization policy enforcement.
This allows for more control over application pods on the same node or different nodes.
The project has also seen the introduction of Kubernetes Gateway API, which is expected to become the new standard for ingress and load balancing.
Additionally, ISTO is working on improving egress controls and observability, with a focus on layer 7 traffic management.
The project's future plans include supporting independent innovation in this space, allowing users to choose from various gateway implementations that meet their specific needs.
