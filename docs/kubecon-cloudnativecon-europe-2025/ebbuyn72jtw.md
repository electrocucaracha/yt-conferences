# Summary

Dan discusses the challenges of authorization in modern applications, highlighting its impact on developer experience and security.
He introduces the concept of authorization as a workflow issue, rather than just a security problem, and proposes a declarative approach to authorization that can be integrated into the development process.
Dan outlines four core design principles for workflow-first authorization: domain-driven authorization, declarative policies, external decision points, and context-aware simple rule-based access control.
He also discusses various tools and patterns, including OPA, Open FGA, Serbos, and policy-driven design, which can help implement these principles.
