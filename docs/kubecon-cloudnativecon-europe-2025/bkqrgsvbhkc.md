# Summary

The speaker, a data engineering expert, discusses their experience with custom schedulers in Kubernetes, particularly with Apache Spark.
They share their journey from using Hadoop to migrating to Kubernetes and how it improved their workflow.
The speaker highlights the benefits of custom schedulers, including efficient infrastructure utilization, cost reduction, and energy savings.
However, they also mention limitations, such as the default scheduler not adapting well to batch workloads and limited multi-tenancy support.
They compare two popular custom schedulers, Volcano and Unicorn, and conclude that both are well-suited for batch workloads but have different complexities and configurations.
The speaker emphasizes the importance of standardization and community adoption in developing effective custom schedulers.
