# Summary

The CNCF is rebranding its network reboot initiative, merging tag networks into a unified framework called "tag infrastructure." This change aims to simplify administrative tasks and reduce the workload on existing chairs and tech leads.
The new setup will include developer experience workflow, operational resilience, security, compliance, and an initiative process for researching and developing patterns for complex networking scenarios like multicluster connectivity.
The CNCF encourages community members to join the tech network, Slack, GitHub, or email to contribute ideas and participate in weekly meetings, which will help reshape the tag reboot and ensure the networking needs of the community are met.
