# Summary

The speaker, Alexandra Stoka, is a site reliability engineer at Adobe who will be discussing debugging Envoy tunnels.
She explains that Envoy is an open-source edge and service proxy designed for cloud-native applications, focusing on service-to-service communication handling, retries, circuit breakers, load balancing, and deep observability.
The presentation includes four scenarios where Envoy fails to communicate properly with clients or other services, including certificate expiration, key-value mismatch, connection issues, and rate limiting.
By increasing logging levels, checking certificates, and examining metrics, attendees can identify the root cause of these failures and take corrective action.
