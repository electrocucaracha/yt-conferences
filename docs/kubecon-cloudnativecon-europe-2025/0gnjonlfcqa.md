# Summary

The Cloud Native Storage and Storage Tag session discussed the importance of cloud-native storage in Kubernetes environments.
The CNCF is reorganizing its tags to focus on five new areas: data, Kubernetes, disaster recovery, messaging, and streaming.
The session also covered the attributes of a storage system, including availability, durability, observability, and elasticity.
It emphasized the need for automation and standardization in cloud-native storage systems, citing examples such as Kubernetes operators and CSI (Container Storage Interface) drivers.
The discussion highlighted the importance of disaster recovery strategies, including backup and restore, volume replication, transactional replication, and active-active deployments.
