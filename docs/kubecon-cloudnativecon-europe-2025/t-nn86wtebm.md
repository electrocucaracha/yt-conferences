# Summary

Shopify's journey with MTLS (Mutual Transport Layer Security) highlights the importance of zero trust adoption and workload identity management for secure service-to-service communication.
The company implemented Spiffy, an industry-standard solution for workload identity, to verify identities and authenticate services.
Shopify also developed a custom tool to manage client certificates, leveraging Prometheus and Graphana for monitoring and alerting, and demonstrated how Argo CD can simplify deployment and access control list management.
