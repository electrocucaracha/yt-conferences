# Summary

The speaker discusses the challenges of building reliable and observable AI agent systems, particularly with the increasing complexity of large language models (LLMs) and vector databases.
They highlight three broad categories of challenges: determinism, signals, and state.
To address these challenges, the speaker introduces the concept of observability for AI agents, which involves tracing prompts, completions, queries, retrieval results, control flow, and framework-level tracing.
The speaker also discusses the importance of semantic conventions, such as those developed by the GenAI Special Interest Group, to standardize the way traces are generated and interpreted.
They provide an overview of the typical setup for instrumenting AI agent systems using open telemetry libraries, third-party projects, or custom instrumentation.
