# Summary

Syan from IBM Research introduces the Kepler project, a research initiative aiming to answer how much energy consumption is attributed to workload at various levels (container, host, metal, etc.).
Launched in February 2022, the project offers three functions: real-time metrics collection, power modeling, and reporting of energy consumptions.
The team has been working on lightweight solutions, leveraging EVPF technologies, and collaborating with Red Hat and other companies to validate power models and expand the project's capabilities.
