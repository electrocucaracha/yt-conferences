# Summary

Majid and Joffra, platform engineers at Spotify's platform developer experience department, discuss how they're using AI to address productivity blockers for their engineers.
They've developed an artificial intelligence knowledge assistant called Aika, which uses semantic search across multiple knowledge sources to provide accurate answers.
The platform has seen wide adoption among 70% of Spotify employees and has helped reduce thousands of hours of work by automating routine questions.
Aika's capabilities are being expanded to include enhanced reasoning and agentic capabilities, allowing it to automatically deduce relevant data sources and act on behalf of users.
