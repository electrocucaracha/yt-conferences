# Summary

The speaker, David Co., is the Engineering Director at SUSE and leads a team working on Longhorn, a distributed bar storage solution based on Kubernetes.
He discusses the project's goals, including promoting Longhorn worldwide, and provides an overview of its features, such as v2 data engines with improved performance, logical volumes, and support for multiple backup modes.
The speaker also highlights upcoming releases, including a new version in May, which will introduce a new release cadency model, allowing for more frequent updates and better expectations for adoption.
David encourages the audience to contribute to the project by deploying Longhorn, providing feedback, or contributing to its development through various channels.
