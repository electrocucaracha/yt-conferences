# Summary

Brent Keller, Technical Lead for Tag Security, discusses the importance of accessibility and diversity in roles within the CNCF landscape.
As projects join the landscape at an increasing rate, it's essential to ensure they meet minimum security thresholds and maintain checks and balances.

Keller emphasizes the need for diverse skill sets and encourages individuals to get involved in enhancing the landscape, with opportunities for contributions ranging from code to documentation efforts.
