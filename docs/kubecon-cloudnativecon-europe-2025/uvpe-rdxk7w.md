# Summary

Decathan, a sports goods retailer, embarked on a journey into platform engineering with the goal of streamlining its digital entity's infrastructure and operations.
Initially, they built a platform engine called 3S, which aimed to provide a standardized way to provision and use infrastructure, but faced limitations such as connectivity issues and high development costs.
After re-evaluating their options, Decathan decided to revert to the 3S engine, acknowledging its familiarity while addressing technical depth and updating its portfolio.
They now focus on refining the link between their developer portal and platform orchestrator to enhance adoption and user experience.
