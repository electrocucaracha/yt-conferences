# Summary

The speaker, Popensson, is the head of container platform engineering at Saxo Bank, which has migrated its critical components from public cloud to on-premises Kubernetes.
The initial motivation was to ensure compliance with regulations and reduce costs, resulting in an 85% saving on compute and storage costs.
The migration process involved replacing HashiCorp's Vault with Sealed Secrets for secret management and customizing the cluster creation speed using Kubernetes API.
While the migration has been relatively smooth, challenges remain, including high availability and routing traffic from outside to inside the cluster, which requires further ironing out.
Saxo Bank's experience suggests that on-premises Kubernetes may not be suitable for all use cases, emphasizing the need for evaluation and consideration of individual circumstances before making a decision.
