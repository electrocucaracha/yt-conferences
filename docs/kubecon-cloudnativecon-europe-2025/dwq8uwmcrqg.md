# Summary

Adun, a Norwegian public sector professional, shares the story of building a platform and community called "Nice" that has transformed the way the government delivers services.
The idea for Nice began at Cubec Con in Berlin 8 years ago, where Adun met AR Arar, who was leading the platform team for the tax authorities in Norway.
They realized there was potential for cooperation and learning from each other and started building a community, which eventually grew to over 2,000 engineers across 86 organizations.
The Nice platform has become a success story, allowing developers to easily build, run, and operate applications with self-service access to a golden path of functionality, and has been adopted by two other government agencies in Norway.
