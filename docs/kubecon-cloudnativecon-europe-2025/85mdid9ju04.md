# Summary

The Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) has reached its 10-year anniversary, with over 12,500 attendees at CubeCon Europe in London.
CNCF was founded in July 2015 with 20 original organizations and has since grown to include over 275,000 contributors across 200 projects from 190 countries.
The organization is celebrating its milestone with various announcements, including the launch of a new education program called Golden Cubstronauts for individuals who have passed multiple CNCF certifications, and a partnership with SAP to create a sovereign cloud edge continuum for Europe through the Neo Nephos initiative.
