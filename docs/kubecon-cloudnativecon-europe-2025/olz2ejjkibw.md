# Summary

The SIG Architecture discussion focuses on the role of architecture in the Kubernetes community, its goals, and its relationship with other SIGs.
The goal is to facilitate technical architectural decisions while adhering to community values.
The SIG works on issues like portability, general-purpose functionality, flexibility, extensibility, and API conventions.
They also address deprecation, production readiness reviews, and code organization to ensure maintainability and security.
The SIG has sub-projects, including conformance testing, enhancements, and production readiness reviews, which work together to improve Kubernetes.
