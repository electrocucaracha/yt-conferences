# Summary

A panel discussion on software supply chain attacks and vulnerabilities was held, featuring experts from Forester Research, Purdue University, and Sonatype.
The conversation centered around the evolution of software supply chain attacks, which have become increasingly sophisticated and widespread.
Experts discussed the difference between supply chain attacks and vulnerabilities, with the former being intentional malicious components and the latter being unintentional bugs that can be exploited.
They also touched on the challenges of detecting and mitigating these threats, including the lack of awareness and understanding among developers and organizations.
The panel highlighted the need for collaboration, information sharing, and the development of new tools to detect and prevent software supply chain attacks.
