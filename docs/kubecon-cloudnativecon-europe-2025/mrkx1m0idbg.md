# Summary

The speaker, Hongang, introduces Kubage, an open-source cloud-native edge computing framework designed for edge-cloud collaboration.
Launched in 2018, Kubage has grown into a mature community with over 8,000 stars and 2,200 forks on GitHub, supported by more than 100 organizations worldwide.
The project has achieved significant milestones, including the graduation of Kubage as a CNCF sandbox project in 2019, its use in large-scale China highway electronic tour connection systems, and the launch of cloud-native vehicles and satellites based on Kubage.
