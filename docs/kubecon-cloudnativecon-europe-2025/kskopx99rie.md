# Summary

The Kubernetes community has grown significantly over the past decade, with 95,000 contributors contributing to the project at any point in time.
The community is structured into three groups: special interest groups (SIGs), working groups, and committees.
SIGs focus on specific aspects of the project, while working groups are short-term groups that address specific goals.
Committees, including the steering committee, code of conduct committee, and product security committee, provide oversight and guidance to the community.
The community also has a mentoring program, which helps new contributors get started and grow in their roles.
To support this growth, the Kubernetes community has established various sub-projects, including contributor experience, community management, and elections.
These efforts aim to improve communication, streamline processes, and foster collaboration among contributors.
