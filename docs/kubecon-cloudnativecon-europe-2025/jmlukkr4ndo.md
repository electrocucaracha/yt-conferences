# Summary

Orland, a CSF ambassador and technology advocate at SUSA, recently took on the role of caretaker for the K3S community.
K3S is a lightweight Kubernetes project under 7 megabytes, designed for IT, IoT, CI/CD, and edge computing, with over 1 million downloads.
Orland aims to reboot the community by engaging with users, gathering feedback, and implementing new ideas, including improving Windows support and adding NATS.
The community will host regular meetings starting next week, where attendees can discuss future developments and share their experiences using K3S.
