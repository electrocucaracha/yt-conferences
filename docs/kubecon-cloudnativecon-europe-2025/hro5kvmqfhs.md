# Summary

Liam Randall, founder and CEO of Cosmonic, discusses platform engineering and its limitations with containers and Kubernetes.
He introduces WebAssembly as a solution to these problems, offering a capability-based approach that allows developers to start with nothing and grant capabilities one at a time.
This enables zero cold starts, small code size, portability across clouds, and pluggable interfaces.
The technology has been adopted by tens of thousands of contributors and is used in various enterprise applications, including those from American Express and the Norwegian Food Safety Agency.
