# Summary

The panel discussion on developer productivity focused on the challenges of measuring and improving individual developer performance, highlighting the importance of considering organizational systems and workflows rather than solely focusing on individual metrics.
The panelists emphasized the need for a more holistic approach to understanding what drives productivity, including factors such as friction, scaffolding, and feedback loops.
They also discussed the value of qualitative data from developers themselves, as well as metrics that can help companies understand the impact of investing in developer experience on bottom-line results.
