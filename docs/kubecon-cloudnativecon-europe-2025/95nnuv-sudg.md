# Summary

This talk by Priyanka Sagu and Jason Banza explores the concept of learning Kubernetes through the lens of metrics, highlighting the importance of understanding what metrics can tell us about our cluster's features, ports, containers, and more.
They demonstrate how to access and interpret various Kubernetes metrics using tools like Prometheus, exploring topics such as feature enablement, port usage, container memory, kernel version, and OS threads, providing hands-on examples and insights into the Kubernetes ecosystem.
