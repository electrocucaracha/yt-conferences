# Summary

The European Union's Cyber Resilience Act (CRA) aims to protect consumers and businesses from cyber security threats in products with digital elements.
The legislation requires manufacturers, open source maintainers, and stewards (those who roll back profits into the open-source model) to cooperate with regulatory bodies and adhere to specific security standards.
Maintainers of open source projects are exempt from liability, but commercial activities involving open source may be subject to regulations.
The Linux Foundation, CNCF, and OpenSSF are working together to make the legislation more accessible and provide tools for compliance, enabling businesses to plan ahead and assume liability for their products.
