# Summary

Andre Jardini, an SRE at Overstory, discussed how his company uses Kubernetes and AI to prevent wildfires.
Wildfires are destructive events that can cause significant loss of life and property.
In California, over 60% of the most destructive wildfires have been caused by defective power lines.
To address this issue, Overstory has developed a platform using Daxter, an open-source workflow engine, to analyze satellite images and detect potential wildfire risks.
The company has also built its own libraries, including Maple, to automate data processing and pipeline management.
With the help of Kubernetes and cloud-native solutions, Overstory aims to improve wildfire prevention and make its delivery process more reliable and efficient.
