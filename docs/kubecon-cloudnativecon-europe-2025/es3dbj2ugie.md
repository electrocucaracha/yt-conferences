# Summary

Matthew Benois, Clative ambassador and customer success engineer at Humanit, is joined by Maxim Kwell, cloud security architect, to discuss platform engineering and its role in simplifying developer life while ensuring security.
They emphasize the importance of a self-service model, automation, and abstraction to reduce friction between developers and platform engineers.
The duo highlights various tools and frameworks, such as CNCF's security model, OASP top 10, and GitLab, GitHub Action, Genkins, for streamlining development processes and improving security.
By adopting a platform engineering mindset, organizations can empower developers while maintaining compliance and stability, ultimately achieving a culture of security that fosters innovation and creativity.
