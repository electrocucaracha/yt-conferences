# Summary

Maxim Vizeno from DataDog's Infrastructure Engineering team discussed their journey to operating infrastructure across three cloud providers (Azure, AWS, and GCP) using Kubernetes.
Initially, they used managed Kubernetes offerings but found them limited in scalability and observability.
To overcome these challenges, the team developed a custom solution that abstracted compute, storage, networking, and database resources, allowing for more efficient management of their infrastructure across multiple cloud providers.
Despite the complexity, the team learned valuable lessons and is now exploring new abstractions and capabilities to further improve their platform's efficiency and scalability.
