# Summary

Novanorisk, a pharmaceutical company, aims to extend Kubernetus with AI capabilities to efficiently process and analyze large amounts of data.
The company has developed an AI platform called RunAI, which provides a user-friendly interface for researchers to deploy and manage machine learning models on their GPU-accelerated cluster, Geon.
The platform is designed to simplify the workflow for researchers, who can now focus on contributing to drug discovery without needing extensive expertise in Kubernetes or DevOps.
By leveraging RunAI, Novanorisk aims to accelerate its research efforts and improve treatment outcomes for various diseases.
