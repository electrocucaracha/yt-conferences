# Summary

The Japanese community has made significant progress in adopting cloud-native technologies, with the establishment of the Cloud Native Community Japan (CNCJ) chapter in 2022.
The CNCJ has successfully organized upstream training, meetups, and conferences, including CubeCon Japan, which aims to bring together global cloud-native experts and Japanese developers.
Despite challenges such as language barriers and time zones, the community has shown resilience and determination to grow and contribute to the global cloud-native ecosystem.
With support from organizations like CNCF and Linux Foundation, CNCJ is poised to become a key player in promoting cloud-native adoption in Japan and beyond.
