# Summary

The Linkerd project has made significant progress over the past year, with a focus on improving its performance, security, and usability.
The team released several new features, including egress rate limiting, federated services, and protocol declarations, which enable more fine-grained control over traffic flow within the mesh.
Additionally, Linkerd is now compatible with Windows environments and has improved support for private networks.
The project's sustainability has also been enhanced due to its adoption by Buoyant, a profitable company that funds the development of Linkerd.
With a roadmap focused on further improving performance, security, and usability, Linkerd continues to be a leading service mesh solution for cloud-native applications.
