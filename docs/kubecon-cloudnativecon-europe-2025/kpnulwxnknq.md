# Summary

The speaker discusses the importance of durable execution and workflow engines in ensuring automatic failure recovery and minimizing the impact of system failures.
They introduce Deer, an open-source project that provides a distributed application runtime, and demonstrate its capabilities through a demo.
The speaker explains how Deer's durable execution concept allows for automatic replay of failed workflows, enabling seamless recovery without human intervention.
They also highlight four key considerations when working with workflows: deterministic code, versioning, argument size, and responsibility distribution among activities.
