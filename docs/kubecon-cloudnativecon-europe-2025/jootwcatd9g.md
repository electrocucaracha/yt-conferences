# Summary

The Kubernetes data protection working group aims to address the limitations of current data protection workflows in Kubernetes.
The group is sponsored by Sik Storage and Sik Apps, and its primary goal is to provide a standardized approach for data protection in Kubernetes.
The working group has identified several strategies for protecting applications, including GitOps backup and restore, replication, and change block tracking.
However, these strategies also come with challenges, such as managing relationships between resources and dealing with dynamic applications.
The working group is currently developing a white paper on overall best practices for Kubernetes applications for data protection, which will provide guidance on selecting the right strategy and implementing it effectively.
