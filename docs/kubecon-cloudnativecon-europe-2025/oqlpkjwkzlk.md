# Summary

The speaker discusses operating global-scale Prometheus deployments on Kubernetes, highlighting challenges such as availability, multi-tenancy, and scalability.
They introduce Cortex, a scalable and open-source project that breaks down Prometheus into microservices, allowing for horizontal scaling and improved deployment safety.
The presentation also covers the concept of cellular architecture, where multiple clusters are deployed across regions, reducing blast radius and improving deployment waves.
The speaker emphasizes testing, including unit tests, acceptance tests, canary tests, fuzziness, and correctness tests, to ensure the stability and performance of Cortex.
