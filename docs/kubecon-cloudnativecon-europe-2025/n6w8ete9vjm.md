# Summary

Hillilary Carter, from the Linux Foundation, discussed fueling cloud-native development by sharing data and insights from their community.
They have a library of 78 unique studies available on Data.World, which can be accessed for free under a community data license agreement 2.0.
The research focuses on industry-specific analysis, technology domains, geographic analysis, and cross-industry issues like developer relations, diversity, equity, and inclusion, cyber security, and standards.
They explored the state of generative AI in cloud-native technologies, finding 84% of organizations have high or very high adoption rates, with Kubernetes being a key component in 50% of use cases for inference workloads.
The foundation is seeking input on data needed to measure contribution ROI, including questions about adoption, standardized metrics, and the value of non-code contributions.
