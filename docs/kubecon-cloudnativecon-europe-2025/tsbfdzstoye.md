# Summary

The speaker discusses the shift towards coalition, standardization, and open telemetry in observability.
They highlight the limitations of traditional approaches, such as relying on humans to correlate signals together, and introduce Open Telemetry, a CNCF project that has become the defacto standard for distributed tracing and now extends to logs and metrics.
The speaker demonstrates how Open Telemetry enables automatic instrumentation, reduces metadata fragmentation, and improves troubleshooting efficiency.
They also showcase Percy's, another CNCF project, which provides an open dashboard specification and eliminates vendor login, allowing teams to define dashboards in YAML and apply them as code.
