# Summary

The company Walter Clure, a large software provider, adopted Open Telemetry to improve monitoring and observability.
They previously relied on multiple vendor tools, leading to complexity and difficulties in resolving incidents.
To address this, they formed a cross-functional team to evaluate different tool options and select one that met their requirements.
The team used an embedded expert model, where platform teams worked with app dev teams to instrument applications with Open Telemetry.
This approach minimized impact on app dev teams and ensured successful adoption.
The company has since rolled out Open Telemetry collector at the edge, enabling centralized processing of telemetry data and improved incident resolution times.
