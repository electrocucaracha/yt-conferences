# Summary

The speaker, Antonio, discusses the challenges of measuring energy consumption in cloud environments, particularly with regards to scalability and abstraction.
He presents a hands-on experiment using a custom-built cluster with four Intel mini PCs, a switch, and a Kubernetes setup, aiming to measure power consumption at different stages: day zero (Kubernetes running), day one (microservices application deployed), and day three (stress testing).
The results show that the actual power consumption is lower than expected, with some spikes due to stress testing.
Antonio highlights the importance of understanding the offset between software measurements and actual energy usage, emphasizing the need for a bottom-up approach to sustainability in cloud environments.
