# Summary

The panel discussion focused on inclusiveness in open-source projects, particularly in the Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF).
The panelists emphasized the importance of creating an inclusive environment that attracts diverse contributors and fosters innovation.
They shared personal experiences of how inclusivity made them feel welcome and valued, such as having captions available during meetings or being able to contribute without feeling overwhelmed.
The panel also discussed ways to support underrepresented contributors, including attending monthly meetings, joining Slack channels, and being an ally by inviting people to initiatives.
Finally, the panel highlighted the importance of recognizing potential and overcoming systemic biases in meritocracy, ensuring opportunities and recognition are based on skills and contributions rather than arbitrary factors.
