# Summary

The speaker, DOM Donano, CEO of Cosmic, discusses expanding EBPF's reach and its applications in observability and security.
He explains how EBPF can provide a system-wide view, allowing for broad coverage, reduced effort, and flexibility.
However, manual instrumentation is often sparse due to third-party application limitations.
EBPF tools like Inspector Gadget, Pulsar, Pixie, and Hubble aim to address this by providing data enrichment, structured APIs, and programmability.
DOM highlights Pixie's ability to process files, combine data from different sources, and provide insights into system behavior, making it a powerful tool for debugging and observability.
