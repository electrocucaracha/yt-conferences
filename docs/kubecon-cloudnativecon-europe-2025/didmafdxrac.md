# Summary

The Cloud Native Computing Foundation (CNCF) hosts community-driven meetups and events to promote cloud-native technologies and foster collaboration among developers.
The organization has chapters around the world, with a focus on vendor neutrality and accessibility.
To grow sustainably, CNCF emphasizes meritocracy in governance, providing opportunities for organizers to develop their skills and contribute to the community.
The foundation also aims to create a more inclusive environment by promoting diversity of speakers, challenges, and networking opportunities.
By sharing best practices and resources, CNCF chapters can learn from each other and improve their events, ultimately benefiting the cloud-native community.
