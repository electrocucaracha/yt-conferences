# Summary

The panel discussion on data management in Kubernetes focused on the growing adoption of AI and ML workloads, with experts sharing their experiences and insights.
Nimisha from Confluent discussed the use of local SSDs for caching, while Brian Kaufman from Google highlighted the importance of caching and parallel downloads for training models.
Nisha from Confluent emphasized the need for hybrid systems to handle real-time data processing and event-driven processing.
The panel also touched on the integration among projects within the CNCF ecosystem, with experts discussing the potential for improved documentation and collaboration.
Additionally, the discussion covered cost management strategies, including proper observability, minimizing latency in GPUs, and using cloud-neutral approaches such as affinity and anti-affinity labels to collocate pods with data processing resources.
