# Summary

The speakers from Bloomberg discuss their experience with Flink on Carmada, an open-source Kubernetes management system that enables resilient data pipelines on multicluster environments.
They share their journey of supporting stateful application failover on Apache Flink and collaborating with the Carmada community to add this support.
The presentation highlights the benefits of a unified control plane, automated cross-cluster failover, and improved operational efficiency, allowing users to manage and deploy jobs from one place with authentication and intelligent scheduling baked in.
