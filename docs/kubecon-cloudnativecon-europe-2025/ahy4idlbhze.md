# Summary

The speakers from Cintaso share their experience building a platform framework for managing Kubernetes clusters across multiple environments.
They learned that "don't reinvent the wheel" by leveraging existing ecosystem tools, such as GitOps, and innovating on top of them.
However, they also realized that going multicluster is complex and requires careful planning.
To mitigate this complexity, they focused on reducing the complexity of things within their control and prioritized user needs with wider organizational goals.
A key takeaway is to never assume users know Kubernetes, as it's a challenging concept for many organizations to grasp.
The speakers emphasize the importance of understanding why you started building in the first place and doing so at the right time.
