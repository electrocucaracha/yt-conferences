# Summary

The panel discussion focused on quantum computing and its integration with Kubernetes, highlighting the need for security updates to accommodate post-quantum cryptography.
The experts emphasized that quantum computing won't replace classical systems but will augment them, and that Kubernetes will play an integral role in managing hybrid workloads.
The challenges of integrating quantum workloads with classical ones include standardization issues, lack of availability of devices, and the need for flexible APIs.
The panelists also discussed the importance of prioritizing high-value data and focusing on cryptography usage to ensure security.
