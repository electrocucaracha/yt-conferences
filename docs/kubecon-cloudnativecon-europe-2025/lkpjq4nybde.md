# Summary

The presentation discusses connecting open-source activities with corporate goals, particularly revenue generation.
The speakers, Kim McMahon and Amanda, share their experiences working in marketing and advocacy roles for open-source projects.
They emphasize the importance of creating a charter to align team members' efforts with company goals, setting clear objectives, and tracking progress through metrics such as OKRs (Objectives and Key Results).
The presentation also highlights the user journey, which involves awareness, consideration, decision-making, and retention phases, and provides examples of how companies have successfully implemented these strategies to drive growth and innovation.
