# Summary

The speaker discusses building an AI serving platform using Kubernetes operators, highlighting the challenges faced and how they overcame them with a design pattern.
They introduce the concept of microcontrollers, which divide complex logic into smaller, manageable pieces, and use Helm templates to connect values and resources.
The platform is designed to be configurable, maintainable, and easy to develop, with clear responsibilities for each person involved, allowing all stakeholders to contribute and understand how they can contribute.
