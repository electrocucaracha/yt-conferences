# Summary

The panel discussion on allyship and accessibility in the cloud-native community was moderated by Katherine Paganini, director of marketing at Buoyant, creator of Linkerd, and co-chair of the Deaf and Heart of Hearing Working Group.
The panelists, including Anastasia Gupska, Shand, Rob Cotch, and Milad, shared their personal experiences with allyship and accessibility, highlighting its impact on their careers and lives.
They emphasized that small actions can make a significant difference and encouraged allies to ask questions, learn about accessibility needs, and support underrepresented groups.
The panelists also discussed the importance of inclusive events, providing resources for accessibility, and expanding localizations to reach deaf and hearing individuals worldwide.
