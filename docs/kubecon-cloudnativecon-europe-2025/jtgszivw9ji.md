# Summary

The panel discussion focuses on adapting Kubernetes to specialized application workloads, particularly AI and machine learning (ML) workloads, and high-performance computing (HPC) nodes.
The experts discuss the challenges of scaling data and ML workloads on Kubernetes, including issues with batch workloads, GPU optimization, and node health.
They also highlight the importance of standardization in observability tools, accelerators, and scheduling parameters.
The conversation touches on the future of cloud computing, the convergence of accelerators, and the need for a single pane of glass across compute resources.
