# Summary

The speaker discusses how the industry's fundamental shift occurred with the introduction of Chat GPT, leading to the development of multimodal AI and enormous volumes of context state.
However, this data became unsustainable and unscaleable, prompting a need for smaller models and distributed computing.
The speaker notes that Kubernetes already provides strong primitives for managing state, but AI is fundamentally different from cloud-native systems, requiring a new paradigm shift towards decentralized AI with intelligent state-aware management.
