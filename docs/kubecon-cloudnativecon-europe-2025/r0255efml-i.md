# Summary

Christine Yen, co-founder and CEO of Honeycomb, discusses the implications of Large Language Models (LLMs) on software development and observability.
She notes that LLMs introduce unpredictability and chaos, making traditional testing methods insufficient.
Observability is key to understanding production behavior, and evaluating (eval) tools help capture intended behavior and flag issues.
To ensure effective observability, Yen advocates for tracking inputs and outputs, capturing metadata, and using spans to isolate interesting behaviors.
She emphasizes the importance of aligning tooling with workflows and not relying on specialized or siloed solutions.
