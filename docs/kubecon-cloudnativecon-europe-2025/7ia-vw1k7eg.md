# Summary

The talk discusses designing APIs for Kubernetes that can evolve over time without breaking changes.
The speakers, Christian and Fitza Pandini, share tips and tricks learned from their experience with Cluster API, including avoiding common mistakes such as embedding external APIs or using generic terms without careful consideration.
They emphasize the importance of choosing accurate keywords in API design to avoid confusion and ensure backwards compatibility.
Additionally, they discuss the benefits of having a glossary for project-specific terminology and encourage attendees to get inspiration from other Kubernetes projects and consider peer reviews and user feedback.
