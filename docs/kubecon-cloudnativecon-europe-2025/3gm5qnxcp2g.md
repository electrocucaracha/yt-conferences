# Summary

George Castra, a CNCF staffer, welcomes attendees to the Project Lightning Talks session, emphasizing the importance of cloud-native technology and its second decade of growth.
He encourages participants to think about the ecology of cloud-native, considering sustainability, contributor health, and project health, as well as integration and collaboration between projects.
The session features lightning talks every 7 minutes, allowing attendees to drop in and out, network, and find maintainers for open-source projects.
