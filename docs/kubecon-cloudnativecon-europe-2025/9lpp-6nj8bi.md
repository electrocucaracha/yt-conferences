# Summary

Camille Fornier, co-author Ian Noland, and their platform engineering team discuss the importance of starting a platform team when scaling to manage complexity, create leverage, and improve productivity.
They emphasize that platform engineering is software engineering and requires software engineers to build abstractions that manage complexity.
The team shares their experiences with building and scaling platforms, highlighting the challenges of managing customer expectations, vendor offerings, and operational burdens.
They also discuss the importance of product thinking, stakeholder communication, and avoiding "builder than they will come" approaches.
