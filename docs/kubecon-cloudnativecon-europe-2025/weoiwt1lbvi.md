# Summary

The LEGO Group, a toy company, has adopted a platform engineering mindset to improve its digital organization.
The team built a new container platform called Novous, which is designed to be highly opinionated and enforce GitOps.
To avoid 50 mediocre Kubernetes implementations, the team created a support rotation where users can ask questions and receive help from dedicated platform engineers.
They also set up workshops and rent-an-engineer programs to engage with users and provide value.
The platform offers a closed and integrated experience, but also provides compliance signoffs and other benefits.
By adopting this approach, the LEGO Group aims to drive adoption of its platform and improve user satisfaction.
