# Summary

The speakers, Vincent (CTO Asia Pacific at Red Hat) and Tamar (IBM Research), discuss the importance of green AI, which focuses on energy efficiency, particularly in cloud-native AI systems.
They highlight the growing energy consumption of deep learning technology, with a 4-5 times annual growth rate since 2010, leading to increased energy demand for data centers.
The speakers propose various techniques to optimize AI inference, including model architecture, deployment and operations, caching, autoscaling, and modularity, emphasizing the need for standardized best practices and community collaboration to achieve significant reductions in energy consumption and costs.
