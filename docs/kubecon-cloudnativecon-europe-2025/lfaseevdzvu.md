# Summary

Gabrielle Kenes from Michan Company, a leader in TI manufacturer, shared their experience of scaling cloud and unPrem infrastructure while cutting costs through Kubernetes.
They initially used a vendor-based solution but faced challenges when the vendor switched its strategy, forcing them to migrate to an open-source platform.
After rebuilding their platform using only open-source software, they implemented a global solution with components such as Aro CD, cluster management, and custom components, resulting in reduced engineering costs (44% year-over-year) and streamlined upgrade processes, while also improving platform engagement among engineers.
