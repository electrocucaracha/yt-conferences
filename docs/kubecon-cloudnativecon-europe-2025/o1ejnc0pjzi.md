# Summary

Prashant, senior DevOps at Morantis, presented a real-world case study on using edge computing with Ker Nuts and Raspberry Pies for coral restoration projects in Vietnam and Mauritius.
The team developed an underwater solution to monitor scientific metrics hourly without human intervention, utilizing a submersible IP68 waterproof enclosure, Raspberry Pi, solar panel, lithium battery, sensors, and a 3G module.
They leveraged Mirantis' open-source project KS (Kubernetes Single Binary) for simple provisioning, deployment, self-healing, and resource limits, achieving stability in the underwater environment.
