# Summary

In the Family Feud-style game show "Kubernetes Family Fortune," two teams, Team Tabs and Team Spaces, compete to guess the most popular responses from a survey of Kubernetes contributors.
The teams are led by Tim Hawin and consist of experts in various fields related to Kubernetes.
Throughout the game, the teams answer questions about Kubernetes features that have caused production incidents, strategies for first-time attendees at KubeCon, and signs that a cluster is becoming self-aware.
In the end, Team Spaces emerges victorious with 114 points, narrowly beating Team Tabs' 113 points.
