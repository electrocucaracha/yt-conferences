# Summary

The speaker, Mache, introduces the SIG Apps group and explains their role in supporting Kubernetes users.
The group focuses on controllers that manage deployments, stateful sets, and jobs, addressing issues such as pod disruption budgets, randomization of rollout order, and job success policies.
They also discuss upcoming features like a backoff limit for jobs and a job pod failure policy.
Additionally, the SIG Apps group is working on architecture node autoscaling and introducing a max available feature for stateful sets, which aims to improve the roll-out process for these applications.
