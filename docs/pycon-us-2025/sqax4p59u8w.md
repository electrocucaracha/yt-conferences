# Summary

At PyCon US 2025, the Diversity and Inclusion panel addressed the challenges of maintaining community belonging amidst recent political and legal changes, emphasizing that inclusion work remains critical despite structural setbacks.
Panelists, including Kenya Phelps, Naomi Cedar, Jay Miller, Christian, and Ola, shared personal experiences of fatigue and defiance, urging the community to move beyond performative outrage toward tangible actions such as financial support for organizations like Black Python Devs and PyLadies.
They highlighted the importance of sustainable inclusion, noting that scalability must respect local capacities, as seen in the differing approaches to supporting PyCon Nigeria versus PyCon Uganda.
The discussion also touched on the value of international solidarity, with Christian explaining how global networks help mitigate the impact of U.S.
DEI rollbacks, while Jay Miller advocated for volunteers to take breaks to prevent burnout and return with fresh energy.
Ultimately, the panelists encouraged attendees to support local meetups, contribute to documentation in diverse languages, and maintain a resilient, optimistic mindset to ensure the Python community continues to thrive and support one another.
