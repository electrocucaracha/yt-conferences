# Summary

Valentina and Laura, two Colombian data professionals, hosted a live podcast recording of their show "Data Soul" at the PyData conference in the United States.
They discussed their transition from telecommunications engineering to data science, highlighting how joining the Data Science FEM community in Medellín provided crucial support and opened doors to new opportunities.
The episode focused on the theme "Changing the Script, We Are Not Alone," emphasizing the importance of community, mentorship, and soft skills in navigating career challenges such as imposter syndrome and the pressure to keep up with rapidly evolving technologies.
They shared personal experiences of overcoming fears related to job applications and client management, noting that while technical skills are essential, effective communication and confidence are equally vital for success.
The session concluded with an audience Q&A addressing gender balance in the industry and the feasibility of career transitions from diverse academic backgrounds, reinforcing the message that community support and self-belief are key to thriving in the data field.
