# Summary

The Python Software Foundation (PSF) announced upcoming board elections for August and September, encouraging community members to run for office, vote, or contribute financially to support the organization.
The next PyCon US conference will be held in Long Beach, California, with a specific initiative to invite Python users from the Hollywood industry to participate.
The event featured the presentation of the Pi Ladies Award, recognizing four individuals for their significant efforts in mentoring underrepresented groups and fostering diversity within the Python community.
Additionally, the PSF presented Community Service Awards to ten individuals for their exemplary contributions to local chapters, conferences, and open-source projects, as well as Distinguished Service Awards to three long-standing leaders for their sustained, high-impact dedication to transforming the PSF into a professional organization.
The session concluded with a reminder to continue supporting newcomers and sponsoring the Python ecosystem to maintain its growth and inclusivity.
