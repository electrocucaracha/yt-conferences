# Summary

At PyCon US, industry leaders from NVIDIA, AWS, Meta, and Google highlighted their ongoing investments in the Python ecosystem, focusing on security, performance, and community growth.
AWS and Alpha Omega detailed their collaborative efforts to enhance supply chain security, while Meta presented new tools like Pyfly and advancements in free-threaded Python to support large-scale AI workloads.
The Python Software Foundation (PSF) reported significant infrastructure growth, including an 84% increase in PyPI downloads, and announced the successful launch of PyPI organizations to support collaborative development.
PSF Executive Director Dev Nicholson emphasized the organization’s mission to protect the language and support its diverse community, acknowledging the challenges of current economic and political climates while celebrating the resilience of Python developers.
The session concluded with Google expressing its commitment to Python’s evolution, particularly in typing standards and performance improvements, underscoring the language’s critical role in modern software development and machine learning.
