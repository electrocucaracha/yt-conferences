# Summary

Maria Ashna, the inaugural PyPI Support Specialist at the Python Software Foundation, presented a talk detailing her efforts to significantly reduce support backlogs and improve user experience.
She highlighted a dramatic improvement in account recovery triage times, which dropped from five months to an average of 1.5 days, and addressed the PEP 541 request backlog, reducing its triage time from over a year to approximately one week.
To support these improvements, Ashna is developing comprehensive internal and user-facing documentation for security workflows, such as two-factor authentication recovery, and is working on a feature to resolve the "typo email loop" that prevents users from verifying accounts with incorrect email addresses.
Additionally, she is collaborating with PyPI admins to establish a standardized process for safely releasing prohibited package names that are no longer associated with malware or dependency confusion.
Finally, she announced upcoming outreach initiatives for PyPI organizations to gather community feedback on desired features, emphasizing a philosophy of using curiosity and discovery to navigate the challenges of being the first person in a new role.
