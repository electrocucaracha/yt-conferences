# Summary

Gabriel Cqel, a developer relations engineer at the Algorand Foundation, presents a talk challenging the common perception that blockchain is primarily used for meme tokens and NFTs.
He argues that blockchain serves as a transformative technology platform capable of solving real-world problems, such as financial exclusion in regions with limited banking infrastructure.
To illustrate this, he highlights Hesape Pay, a mobile payments platform operating in Afghanistan that leverages the Algorand blockchain to facilitate transactions via QR codes, overcoming the country’s cash shortages and lack of traditional banking.
The speaker also discusses Travel X, which tokenizes airline tickets to enable peer-to-peer resale, and Lofty, a company that fractionalizes real estate into tradable digital assets to lower investment barriers.
Throughout the presentation, Cqel demonstrates how developers can build these applications using Python and the Algo Kit framework, emphasizing Algorand’s features like instant finality, low transaction costs, and strong Python support for creating secure, decentralized solutions.
