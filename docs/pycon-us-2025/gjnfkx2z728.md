# Summary

Quinn, a staff scientist at SLAC National Accelerator Laboratory, presented a collaborative effort with NVIDIA to accelerate scientific data computation using GPU computing platforms.
The talk focused on utilizing X-ray free Electron lasers (XFELs) to study material science, specifically how stacking different material layers can engineer electronic properties for advanced applications like quantum technologies and fusion energy.
A major challenge in this field is the massive volume of data generated by experiments, which previously limited real-time analysis to only 10% of the dataset due to computational constraints.
By implementing CuPy, a NumPy-compatible library for GPUs, the team achieved implicit parallelism that reduced full detector analysis time from over ten hours to just four minutes.
This significant speedup, reaching up to 180 times faster than CPU-based methods, enables live feedback during experiments and drastically compresses the time required for data processing and publication, ultimately enhancing discovery science across various fields.
