# Summary

The Community Awards ceremony recognized outstanding contributions to the CNCF community.
The awards honored individuals who have made significant impacts in various areas, including documentation, mentorship, technical advisory groups, and fighting patent trolls.
Notable winners included Raphael Gonalves for his work at Michelin, Lee Kalcote for exceptional mentoring, John Howard for his contributions to Kubernetes, and Don Chang and Kevin Wang for their lifetime achievement.
The ceremony celebrated the community's playful nature and the value of individual contributions to the CNCF ecosystem.
