# Summary

The video discusses the implementation of AI in production, specifically in the context of security vulnerability remediation.
The speakers, Benji Kalman and Shiran Milammed, introduce JFrog, a platform that handles challenges such as managing binaries and images, security aspects, machine learning, and more.
They also present Argo workflows, which enable the orchestration of complex workflows with AI agents, reducing time spent on vulnerability remediation from 14-75 hours to just a few minutes.
The speakers emphasize the importance of separating concerns between teams, using defined APIs and contracts, and monitoring workflows and agent processes separately.
