# Summary

The Cyber Resilience Act in the EU aims to regulate software used in devices and products, requiring a list of "ingredients" (software) to be safe, up-to-date, and non-vulnerable.
As an open-source developer or maintainer, you don't have to worry about this unless your software is used commercially, making it a manufacturer.
In that case, you'll need to report security issues and follow guidelines.
The law has already gone into force in the EU but won't be enforced until next year, giving developers time to comply with best practices and standards being developed by organizations like the Linux Foundation and OSSF.
