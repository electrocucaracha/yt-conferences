# Summary

Kashif Khan, co-chair of CNCF tag infrastructure and open source architect at Ericen software technology, updates on Metal Cube.io, a project that brings Kubernetes-style lifecycle management to bare metal servers.
As an official CNCF incubating project, it recognizes the project's maturity and growth, thanks to contributors and community members.
Metal Cube enables users to deploy Kubernetes clusters on bare metal servers by providing a YAML representation and reconciling it with a controller, which handles provisioning and deprovisioning of hosts.
The project has seen significant community engagement, adoption by major companies, and recent updates, including support for the V1 beta 2 API and improvements in IPAM and DHCP provisioning.
