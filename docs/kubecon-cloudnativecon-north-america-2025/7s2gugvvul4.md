# Summary

The panel discussion on cloud-native resilience and accessibility featured experts from various backgrounds, including deafness and visual impairment.
The conversation centered around the importance of inclusion in the tech industry, with topics such as AI-powered tools for accessibility, asynchronous communication, and the need for open-minded listening to create more inclusive teams.
Panelists shared their personal experiences and insights on how to improve team efficiency and make workplaces more welcoming for individuals with disabilities.
