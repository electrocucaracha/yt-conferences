# Summary

Shri and Kate, senior product manager and engineer at Spotify respectively, share their story of building the scorecard plugin, a tool designed to provide a unified experience for internal SAS and on-prem adopters.
The plugin was initially built to address scalability issues with 30,000+ software components, but faced challenges in balancing commercial ambitions with technical realities.
They learned that building a successful product requires intentional alignment between product and tech goals, prioritization through the ICE model, and continuous adaptation to user needs and market changes.
