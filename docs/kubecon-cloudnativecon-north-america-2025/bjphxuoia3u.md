# Summary

The Zinga team from Take Two Interactive discussed their approach to launching games on Kubernetes.
They operate in a hub-and-spoke model, providing common services such as identity and game state management to multiple mobile games.
The team manages over 20 customer clusters and has over 220 Kubernetes users within the company.
During game launches, they work closely with customers to determine initial tuning, perform load testing, and ensure sufficient capacity to handle peak player engagement.
They also have a dedicated incident response process in place, which includes a shared responsibility model and a strict policy for updating stakeholders during incidents.
The team's favorite aspect of KubeCon was the opportunity to connect with others who understand their work, with many finding it refreshing to be surrounded by people who "get" what they do.
