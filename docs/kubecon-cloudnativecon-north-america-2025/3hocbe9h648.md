# Summary

The speaker discusses the CNCF project, which uses WebAssembly for AI inference on server-side, making it cross-platform and suitable for various devices and architectures.
The project has collaborated with Samsung to integrate its technology into their Android phones, including the Gaia phone, and has recently released a major update with compiler-level support for array, struct, ref, and GC instructions.
Future development plans include releasing version 0.16.0 and supporting WASM 3.0, as well as enhancing type safety and validators.
The project is fully open-source and built with Rust, allowing users to assemble their own voice AI agents at home.
