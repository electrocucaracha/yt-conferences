# Summary

Ali Shandri, a software engineer with 18 years of experience, introduces herself as part of Ital, Latin America's largest bank, which has been modernizing its IT infrastructure to deliver value to customers while ensuring sustainability and competitiveness.
The bank has successfully migrated 50% of its services to the cloud by 2022, aiming for 100% by 2028.
To improve developer experience, they created a platform with four tenants: developer journey, product experimentation, standardization, and runtimes.
This platform enables velocity, quality, security, and delivery of business value, while also addressing pain points such as time-consuming outer loops, low feature delivery, and high operational overhead.
