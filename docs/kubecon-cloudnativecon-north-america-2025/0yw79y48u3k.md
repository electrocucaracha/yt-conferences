# Summary

In this presentation, Anup and Jerome from Guidewire Software discuss their journey in building internal development platforms on Kubernetes, focusing on democratizing platform engineering through a component contributor architecture.
They explain the challenges faced by platform engineering teams, such as long backlogs and the expectation to support every cloud service, and propose shifting responsibility by enabling application teams to contribute and manage their own components within defined guardrails.
The team adopted the Open Application Model and implemented KubeVela to provide high-level abstractions, allowing developers to declaratively compose applications using reusable components and traits, thus simplifying deployment, scaling, and operational concerns.
Through demos, they illustrate how this approach unifies application topology and deployment, streamlines documentation, and supports multi-cloud environments, while also establishing a shared responsibility model where experts maintain components and documentation is automatically generated.
The session concludes with lessons learned about balancing security and access, and an invitation for further discussion and open source contributions.
