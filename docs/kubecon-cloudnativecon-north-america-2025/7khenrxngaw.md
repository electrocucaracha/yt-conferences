# Summary

Mora Kelly, director of engineering at Init, discussed the company's transition of Mailchimp onto its developer platform built on open-source technologies.
The move aimed to improve efficiency, scalability, and compliance.
Steve Bower from Bloomberg shared their experience with Kubernetes, starting in 2016, and how it enabled them to build large-scale distributed systems and make AI infrastructure more accessible.
Adam Kokalowski from Airbnb discussed the company's use of CNCF projects, including scheduling and orchestration, CI/CD, and AI tools, which have improved their platform engineering efforts.
Lee Guang from Biden's infrastructure team introduced the open-source project AI Bricks, designed to run large model inference reliably and efficiently in a Kubernetes-native way, with over 4,000 GitHub stars and nearly 100 contributors since its release.
